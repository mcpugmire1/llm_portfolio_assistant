## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work.

At JP Morgan Chase, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation from August 2009 to June 2012. The bank was consolidating regional treasury and cash-management systems into a unified global portal. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but its out-of-box capabilities did not cover the bank’s payment, security, and workflow requirements. Legacy systems also had different data models, entitlement structures, and transaction histories, and live payments had to continue throughout the transition.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and JP Morgan Chase resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the United States. He defined the onshore-offshore engagement model and served as the primary technical interface with Bottomline, spending 70 to 80% of his time embedded at its Portsmouth offices. He coordinated with bank stakeholders in Chicago and New York/New Jersey, working with enterprise architects, security specialists, compliance teams, and developers to determine where WebSeries configuration ended and custom engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and tax-payment modules, including payment verification through Message Authentication Code security controls. He also designed the centralized gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and payment routing across 12 countries, connecting ACCESS to clearing networks and internal compliance, settlement, and reporting systems.

For corporate administrators, he replaced individual permission configuration with role-based access controls, bulk changes, and centralized visibility. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes. He also led payments data migration, preserving client configurations, permissions, transaction histories, and compliance traceability without disrupting live payment operations. During production incidents, he directed cross-team root-cause analysis and ran status updates and daily leadership calls.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ corporate, financial institution, government, and municipal clients across 180+ countries. The gateway gave treasurers a single entry point for payments across regions, replacing separate local logins and fragmented integrations.
