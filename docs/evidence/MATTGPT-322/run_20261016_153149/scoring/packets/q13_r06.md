## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead on ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified global corporate banking portal. Its foundation, Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform, needed custom engineering for 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries. Regional payment systems had different formats, client configurations, entitlement structures, and regulatory requirements, all while live payment processing had to continue.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and JP Morgan Chase resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth, New Hampshire offices, managing where native platform capabilities ended and bespoke engineering began.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and government tax payment modules, including NACHA file validation, foreign-exchange engine integration, and Message Authentication Code security for payment verification. He also delivered the centralized payments gateway: message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries, connecting ACCESS with clearing networks and internal compliance, settlement, and reporting systems.

The work included payment-type-specific permissions, such as allowing wire approval but not FX initiation. Bulk permission changes and role-based profiles reduced entitlement management from days to minutes. Matt also mapped jurisdiction-specific requirements into data visibility and access controls, coordinating regional launches around regulatory readiness rather than a single global deployment.

ACCESS launched in April 2011. The gateway gave corporate treasurers a single entry point for payments across regions. Legacy client configurations, entitlements, and transaction histories were migrated without disruption to live payment operations, and regional launches proceeded without regulatory blockers. Matt also owned production incident response, coordinating root-cause analysis across the bank, offshore teams, and Bottomline.
