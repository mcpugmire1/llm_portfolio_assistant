## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified corporate banking portal. The challenge went beyond replacing screens: regional payment systems had different formats, client configurations, entitlement structures, and compliance requirements. Corporate treasurers navigated separate regional systems, while administrators spent days manually configuring user permissions. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed custom engineering beyond its out-of-box capabilities.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices. He designed and delivered payment modules for ACH, wires, FX/cross-border transfers, and federal tax payments, including payment verification, compliance validation, and payment-type-specific access controls. Contextual help explained wire fields, tax codes, and FX parameters within the payment screens.

He also designed the centralized payments gateway, handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries. Corporate treasurers gained a single entry point for initiating payments across regions instead of separate local logins. For client administrators, Matt introduced role-based profiles, bulk permission changes, and a centralized dashboard, reducing entitlement management from days to minutes.

Alongside that engineering, Matt led payments data migration without disruption to live payment operations. He mapped jurisdiction-specific requirements into data visibility and access controls, sequencing regional launches by regulatory readiness. He also owned production incident response and coordinated root-cause analysis across the bank, offshore teams, and Bottomline.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, consolidating regional systems into a single portal serving 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries. The regional compliance framework enabled launches across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas without regulatory blockers.
