## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for payments on ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s global corporate banking portal. Its treasury services ran through separate regional and legacy systems. Corporate treasurers faced fragmented payment channels, while administrators spent days manually configuring permissions. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment modules, security controls, and workflows beyond its out-of-box capabilities.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, working through where native platform functionality ended and custom engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and federal tax payment modules, along with payment verification through Message Authentication Codes. For corporate users, he added contextual help explaining wire fields, tax codes, and FX requirements directly within payment workflows. He also delivered payment-type-specific permissions, allowing someone to approve wires without being authorized to initiate FX payments.

Alongside the payment engine, Matt designed the centralized gateway connecting payment channels across 12 countries, handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing. He led payments data migration while preserving client configurations, entitlement structures, transaction histories, and compliance traceability. For regional rollout, he translated jurisdiction-specific requirements into data visibility and access controls, sequencing launches by regulatory readiness.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ corporate, financial institution, government, and municipal clients across 180+ countries. Treasurers gained a single entry point instead of separate regional logins. Bulk permission changes reduced entitlement administration from days to minutes. Legacy data migration completed without disruption to live payment operations, and regional launches proceeded without regulatory blockers.
