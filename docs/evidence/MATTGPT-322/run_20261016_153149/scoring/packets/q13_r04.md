## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for payments on ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s corporate banking portal. The challenge was more than replacing a user interface: regional payment systems had different data models, entitlements, message formats, and regulatory requirements. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment capabilities and security controls beyond its out-of-box offering.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and JP Morgan Chase resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, working directly with the vendor to determine where native platform capabilities ended and bespoke engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and federal tax payment modules. The work included validating NACHA files, integrating the bank’s foreign exchange engine, and implementing Message Authentication Code security so corporate users could verify payment integrity before release. He also built payment-type-specific permissions, such as allowing wire approval without FX initiation, and contextual help explaining payment fields and compliance requirements.

Alongside the payment engine, Matt designed the centralized gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries. Instead of retaining fragmented point-to-point integrations, the gateway connected ACCESS with clearing networks and internal compliance, settlement, and reporting systems.

He also led payments data migration, preserving client configurations, transaction histories, and compliance traceability without disruption to live payment operations. His entitlement redesign introduced role-based profiles and bulk permission changes, reducing administration from days to minutes. During rollout and subsequent releases, he owned production support and severe-incident response.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, consolidating regional systems into a unified portal serving 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries. Its payments gateway gave corporate treasurers a single entry point for wire, ACH, and cross-border transfers across regions.
