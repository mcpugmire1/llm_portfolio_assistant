## Visitor: How did Matt build the AWS certification program?

### A

🐾 Found it!

At Accenture’s Liquid Studio, traditional training programs couldn’t keep pace with rapidly evolving AWS technologies. Engineers had gaps in cloud competency, and cloud adoption was slowing. The AWS Launchpad program was designed to build deep cloud competencies, certify engineers, and prepare teams for cloud-native transformation initiatives.

Matt led Launchpad at the Atlanta Innovation Hub, working in collaboration with AWS. Rather than relying on theory-based training, he combined structured learning paths with interactive workshops, hands-on labs, and expert mentorship. Engineers worked in real-world AWS environments so they could apply what they were learning.

He designed the learning paths and certification programs around AWS core services, including Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate, alongside cloud-native application development. The program supported certifications including AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer.

Matt also established cloud expert mentorship teams to embed best practices and retain institutional knowledge. Group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops gave engineers opportunities to learn together, with mentorship supporting continued competency growth beyond individual training sessions. Certification preparation sat alongside practical application, rather than replacing it.

More than 200 professionals earned AWS certifications through Launchpad. The program accelerated cloud adoption and the launch of cloud-native initiatives at Accenture.

In a separate program, Matt coordinated the AWS technology training track for Accenture’s annual ARISE HBCU Conference. He assembled curriculum and presenters, secured keynote speakers, personally delivered cloud computing and AWS fundamentals sessions, and coordinated free AWS certificate training opportunities. More than 250 students and recent graduates participated each year across 4 years. That participation figure is separate from Launchpad’s certification results.
