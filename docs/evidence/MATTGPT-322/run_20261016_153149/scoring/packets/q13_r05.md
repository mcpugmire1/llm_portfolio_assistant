## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s global corporate banking portal. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment capabilities well beyond its out-of-box offering. Regional systems had different data models, entitlements, and compliance requirements, while aggressive delivery timelines ran alongside live payment operations.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He defined the onshore-offshore engagement model and spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, coordinating with bank stakeholders in Chicago and NY/NJ. With Bottomline, enterprise architects, security specialists, and development teams, he determined where platform configuration ended and custom engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and federal tax payment modules, alongside Message Authentication Code security, payment-type-specific permissions, and contextual help. He also designed the centralized gateway connecting payment channels across 12 countries, handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing into settlement and compliance systems.

The work extended through migration and rollout. Matt coordinated legacy-data verification with compliance teams, introduced compliance dashboards, and sequenced regional launches around regulatory readiness. He owned production incident response, coordinating root-cause analysis and daily leadership calls. The redesigned entitlement engine replaced individual permission changes with bulk grouping and role-based profiles, reducing administration from days to minutes.

ACCESS launched in April 2011 as a unified portal serving 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries. The gateway replaced fragmented regional integrations, and the legacy-data migration completed without disruption to live payment operations. Regional compliance configurations enabled launches across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas without regulatory blockers.
