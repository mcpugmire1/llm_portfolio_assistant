## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified corporate banking portal. Treasury operations ran on separate regional and legacy systems, with different data models, payment formats, entitlements, and compliance requirements. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed custom engineering for 135,000+ clients across 180+ countries.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across the US, Mumbai, and Bangalore. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, managing the boundary between native platform capabilities and bank-specific development.

He designed and delivered payment modules for ACH credits and direct debits, Fedwire and international wires, FX/cross-border payments, and federal tax payments. The work included NACHA file validation, foreign exchange rate visibility, payment-integrity verification through Message Authentication Codes, and payment-type-specific permissions. He also delivered the centralized gateway handling message transformation, protocol translation, and payment routing across 12 countries.

The administration work replaced individual permission configuration with role-based profiles and bulk changes across users, accounts, and products. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes. For the regional rollout, Matt translated jurisdiction-specific requirements into data visibility and access controls, sequencing launches around regulatory readiness rather than a single global deployment.

Matt also led payments data migration, preserving client configurations, entitlement structures, transaction histories, and compliance traceability without disruption to live payment operations. He owned production incident response and coordinated root-cause analysis across the bank, offshore teams, and Bottomline.

ACCESS launched in April 2011. Corporate treasurers gained a single entry point for wire, ACH, and cross-border transfers across regions, replacing separate local logins and regional payment systems. The platform launched across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas without regulatory blockers.
