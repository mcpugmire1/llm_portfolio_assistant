## Visitor: How did Matt build the AWS certification program?

### A

🐾 Here’s how Matt built AWS Launchpad.

At Accenture’s Liquid Studio, traditional training programs weren’t keeping pace with rapidly evolving AWS technologies. Engineers had gaps in cloud competency, and cloud adoption was slowing. Teams needed practical experience with cloud-native application development, not just more theory.

Matt led the AWS Launchpad program at the Atlanta Innovation Hub, developed in collaboration with AWS. The goal was to certify engineers while preparing them to lead cloud-native transformation initiatives.

He designed structured learning paths and certification programs covering AWS core services, including Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate. Instead of relying on classroom instruction alone, he delivered interactive workshops and hands-on labs that immersed engineers in real-world AWS environments. Participants could practice applying the services they were learning rather than treating certification preparation as a separate theoretical exercise.

Matt also established cloud expert mentorship teams so engineers had people to turn to as they developed their skills. He organized group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops, giving participants opportunities to learn together and making cloud expertise available beyond individual training sessions. The program combined structured study, practical application, and ongoing mentor support.

More than 200 professionals earned AWS certifications, including AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer certifications. Engineers gained credentials and practical cloud experience, while Accenture accelerated cloud adoption and the launch of cloud-native initiatives. The mentorship teams and shared learning sessions supported continued learning beyond the certification program.
