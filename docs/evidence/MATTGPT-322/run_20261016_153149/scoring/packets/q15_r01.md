## Visitor: Why hire Matt?

### A

🐾 Here’s what Matt has built, changed, and delivered.

Matt’s work spans execution and delivery, organizational transformation, and talent development. At Accenture, clients wanted to move from waterfall delivery and feature factories to iterative, customer-centric product development. That meant changing how teams worked while accounting for legacy systems, compliance requirements, and enterprise governance. At Capital One, slow, misaligned product iterations were extending time-to-market and disconnecting solutions from user needs. As Accenture expanded its Cloud Innovation Centers, many new hires also lacked hands-on experience with cloud-native technologies or Agile practices.

Matt built the Atlanta and Tampa Cloud Innovation Centers from the ground up, owning full P&L accountability, hiring, training, and practitioner development. He brought product managers, designers, and engineers together in balanced teams. He adapted Lean XP for enterprise complexity, integrating pair programming, test-driven development, and automated delivery pipelines with security controls and governance checkpoints.

At Capital One, he built a cross-functional team of 8 around a client-prioritized, well-written, testable backlog. The team practiced disciplined TDD and BDD, following red-green-refactor: write a failing test, write minimal code to pass it, then safely refactor. They delivered features 3 to 4x faster than other Accenture teams on the same engagement, with zero production defects. The client sought to retain the team beyond the planned engagement.

Matt also established tailored onboarding, mentoring, skills frameworks, and an “I do, we do, you do” learning model. Practitioners progressed from guided to independent delivery, with near-zero attrition in the first 2 years.

The centers grew from 0 to 150+ practitioners, delivering 30+ product engagements across 15+ Fortune 500 clients. CIC teams achieved 4X faster velocity and zero-defect deployments. Delivery excellence and client advocacy helped generate \$100M+ in repeatable business by FY23.
