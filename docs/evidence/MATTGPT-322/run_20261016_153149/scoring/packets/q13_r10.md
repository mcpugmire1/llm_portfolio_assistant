## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s global corporate banking portal. The bank’s regional payment systems had different data models, client configurations, entitlement structures, and compliance requirements. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment capabilities well beyond its out-of-box offering.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and JP Morgan Chase resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He defined the onshore-offshore engagement model and spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth, New Hampshire offices. He worked directly with the vendor on where native platform capabilities ended and bank-specific engineering was required, while coordinating with bank stakeholders in Chicago and NY/NJ.

His delivery responsibilities included ACH credits and direct debits, wire payments, FX/cross-border payments, and federal tax payments. He also designed the centralized payments gateway, handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries. Payment verification used Message Authentication Code security controls; payment-type-specific permissions could authorize someone to approve wires without permitting FX initiation.

Matt brought Bottomline, enterprise architects, security specialists, regional compliance officers, and developers together to define access rules and jurisdiction-specific data visibility. He sequenced regional launches around regulatory readiness rather than a single global deployment. His team also replaced individual permission configuration with bulk changes across users, accounts, and products, reducing entitlement management from days to minutes.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries. The payments migration completed without disruption to live payment operations. Matt also owned production incident response, coordinating root-cause analysis across bank, offshore, and vendor teams and running status updates and daily leadership calls while incidents were live.
