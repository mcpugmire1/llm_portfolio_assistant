## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase centered on ACCESS Next Generation, its unified global corporate banking portal.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead. The bank’s regional payment systems had different data models, client configurations, entitlement structures, and compliance records. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment capabilities beyond its out-of-box offering for 135,000+ clients across 180+ countries.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth, New Hampshire offices, working through where native platform capabilities ended and custom engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and federal tax payment modules, including payment formatting, validation, and compliance requirements. He also built the Message Authentication Code security layer for payment-integrity verification. For the centralized payments gateway, he designed message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across payment networks in 12 countries, connecting ACCESS to internal compliance, settlement, and reporting systems.

The work also covered administration and rollout. Matt introduced role-based access controls, payment-type-specific permissions, and bulk permission changes, reducing entitlement management from days to minutes. He led payments data migration without disruption to live payment operations, preserving client configurations, entitlements, transaction histories, and compliance traceability.

Across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas, he translated jurisdiction-specific requirements into data-visibility rules, access controls, and platform configuration. Regional launches were sequenced by regulatory readiness rather than a single global deployment.

ACCESS launched in April 2011. Corporate treasurers gained a single entry point for payments across regions, replacing separate local logins and fragmented regional payment systems. Matt also owned on-call and critical production incident response across releases and migrations.
