### B

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified corporate banking portal. The challenge was more than replacing a user interface: regional payment systems had different data models, connectivity, entitlements, and regulatory requirements. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment capabilities beyond its out-of-box offering.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, working through where native platform capabilities ended and bespoke engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and tax payment modules, each with distinct validation, formatting, settlement, and compliance requirements. He also built payment verification through Message Authentication Codes and payment-type-specific permissions, such as allowing wire approval without allowing FX initiation. For the global payments gateway, he designed message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries, connecting ACCESS to clearing networks and internal compliance, settlement, and reporting systems.

Rather than retain individual permission configuration, Matt introduced role-based profiles and bulk changes across users, accounts, and products. Entitlement management fell from days to minutes. He also led legacy payment-data migration without disruption to live payment operations, preserving client configurations, transaction histories, and compliance traceability.

For regional rollout, Matt translated local regulatory requirements into data-visibility and access controls, sequencing launches by regulatory readiness rather than using a single global deployment. He also owned production incident response and cross-team root-cause analysis.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, consolidating regional systems into a single portal serving 135,000+ corporate, financial institution, government, and municipal clients across 180+ countries.
