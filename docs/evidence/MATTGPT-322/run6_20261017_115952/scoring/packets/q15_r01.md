## Visitor: Why hire Matt?

### A

🐾 Here’s what Matt has built and delivered.

Matt’s work spans Execution & Delivery, Org & Working-Model Transformation, and Talent & Enablement. At Accenture, clients were struggling with slow development cycles, fragmented workflows, and legacy systems. They wanted to move from waterfall and feature factories to iterative, customer-centric product development, but that meant changing how product managers, designers, and engineers worked together, not just introducing new tools.

Matt built the Atlanta and Tampa Cloud Innovation Centers from 0 to 150+ practitioners, with full P&L accountability, hiring, training, and career development. He adapted Lean XP for enterprise compliance, security, and legacy systems. Instead of siloed functions handing work off, he established balanced teams combining product, engineering, and design. Instead of leaving quality to a separate QA phase, teams used pair programming, test-driven development, and automated pipelines.

At Capital One, slow, misaligned product iterations were extending time-to-market. Matt introduced a client-prioritized, testable backlog and disciplined red-green-refactor: write a failing test, write minimal code to pass it, then safely refactor. The 8-person team delivered features 3 to 4x faster than other Accenture teams on the same engagement, with zero production defects. Capital One sought to retain the team beyond the planned end.

He also built the training behind delivery. New hires who lacked hands-on cloud-native or Agile experience progressed through an “I do, we do, you do” learning model, supported by mentors and tailored training. The centers achieved near-zero attrition in the first 2 years.

Across the centers, teams delivered 30+ product engagements for 15+ Fortune 500 clients. Delivery excellence and customer relationship building generated \$100M+ in repeat business, and client teams continued using the practices after engagements ended.
