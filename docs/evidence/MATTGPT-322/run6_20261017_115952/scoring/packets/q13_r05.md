## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Found it!

Matt worked on JP Morgan Chase’s ACCESS Next Generation payments platform from August 2009 to June 2012, serving as Technology Delivery Lead and Senior Technology Architecture Manager. The bank was consolidating regional treasury systems into a single global portal, but Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform did not provide all the payment modules, security controls, or workflows its corporate clients required. Each payment type had distinct formatting, validation, compliance, and approval requirements.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, working through where the platform’s native capabilities ended and bespoke engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and tax payment modules. These included NACHA file validation, integration with the bank’s foreign exchange engine, and Message Authentication Code security so corporate users could verify payment integrity before release. He also built contextual help into payment screens, explaining wire fields, tax codes, and FX parameters within the workflow.

Alongside the payment engine, Matt designed the centralized gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries, replacing fragmented point-to-point integrations. He introduced bulk entitlement changes across users, accounts, and products from a single screen, replacing individual permission configuration and reducing administration from days to minutes.

For migration and rollout, he coordinated legacy-data verification, preserved compliance traceability, and mapped jurisdiction-specific requirements into data visibility and access controls. Regional launches were sequenced by regulatory readiness rather than a single global deployment.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ corporate, financial institution, government, and municipal clients across 180+ countries. The legacy migration completed without disruption to live payments, and the regional compliance framework enabled rollout across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas without regulatory blockers.
