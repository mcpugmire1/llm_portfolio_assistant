## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s global corporate banking portal. Its treasury operations ran through separate regional systems, while Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform needed custom engineering for the bank’s payment, security, and workflow requirements. Corporate treasurers faced fragmented payment systems and separate local logins. Client administrators spent days manually configuring user permissions.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, working through where WebSeries’ native capabilities ended and bank-specific development was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and tax payment modules, including NACHA file validation, payment integrity verification through Message Authentication Codes, and payment-type-specific permissions. Contextual help explained wire fields, tax codes, and FX requirements directly within payment workflows. He also delivered the centralized gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries.

For client administrators, Matt replaced individual permission changes with role-based profiles and bulk changes across users, accounts, and products from a single screen. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes. He also led payments data migration without disruption to live payment operations, preserving client configurations, entitlements, transaction histories, and compliance traceability.

For the regional rollout, he translated jurisdiction-specific requirements into data visibility and access controls, sequencing launches around regulatory readiness. He owned on-call and critical incident response, coordinating root-cause analysis and live status updates.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, consolidating regional systems into a unified portal serving 135,000+ clients across 180+ countries. Corporate treasurers gained a single entry point for payments across regions, with global cash reporting and single sign-on across treasury functions.
