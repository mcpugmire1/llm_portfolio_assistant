## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified corporate banking portal. The starting point was fragmented: regional payment systems had different data models, client configurations, message formats, and compliance requirements. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment capabilities and controls well beyond its out-of-box offering.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across the US, Mumbai, and Bangalore. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, managing the boundary between native platform capabilities and bank-specific engineering.

He designed and delivered ACH payments for payroll, suppliers, and collections; wire payments covering Fedwire, international wires, book transfers, and drawdowns; and FX/cross-border and federal tax payments. He also implemented Message Authentication Code security, payment-type-specific permissions, sanctions screening, and integration with transaction monitoring.

Alongside the payment engine, Matt designed the centralized gateway handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries. Corporate treasurers gained a single entry point for wire, ACH, and cross-border transfers rather than separate regional logins.

The supporting work included replacing individual permission configuration with role-based profiles and bulk changes, reducing entitlement management from days to minutes. Matt also led payments data migration without disruption to live payment operations, preserving client configurations, entitlements, transaction histories, and compliance traceability. For the regional rollout, he translated local regulatory requirements into data visibility and access controls, sequencing launches by regulatory readiness.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, consolidating regional systems into a portal serving 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries. The regional compliance framework enabled launches across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas without regulatory blockers.
