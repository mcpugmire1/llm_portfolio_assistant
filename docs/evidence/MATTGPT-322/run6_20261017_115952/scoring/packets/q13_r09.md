## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified corporate banking portal. The starting point was fragmented: regional payment systems had different data models, client configurations, entitlements, and connectivity. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment capabilities and controls beyond its out-of-box offering.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across the US, Mumbai, and Bangalore. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, working through where native platform capabilities ended and bespoke engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and federal tax payment modules. That included NACHA file validation, embedded FX rate visibility, payment-type-specific permissions, and a Message Authentication Code security layer so corporate users could validate payment integrity before release. He also built contextual help directly into payment screens, explaining wire fields, tax codes, and cross-border requirements.

Alongside the payment engine, Matt designed the centralized gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries. For administrators, he introduced bulk permission changes across users, accounts, and products from a single screen, reducing entitlement management from days to minutes.

Matt also led payments data migration without disruption to live payment operations and coordinated jurisdiction-specific data visibility and access controls. Regional launches were sequenced by regulatory readiness rather than a single global deployment. He owned production incident response, including root-cause analysis and live leadership updates.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, consolidating regional systems into a single portal serving 135,000+ corporate, financial institution, government, and municipal clients across 180+ countries. The gateway gave treasurers a single entry point for wire, ACH, and cross-border transfers across regions.
