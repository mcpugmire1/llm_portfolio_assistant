## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s what Matt built for JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for payments on ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s global corporate banking portal. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment modules, security controls, and approval workflows beyond its out-of-box capabilities. Regional systems had different data models and entitlement structures, while payment connectivity across 12 countries involved different formats, protocols, and regulatory requirements.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across the US, Mumbai, and Bangalore. He spent 70-80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, determining where platform configuration ended and bespoke engineering was required. He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and federal tax payment capabilities, including NACHA file validation, foreign-exchange integration, and Message Authentication Code security so corporate users could verify payment integrity before release.

He also designed the centralized payments gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and routing. For administrators, he replaced individual permission configuration with role-based profiles and bulk changes across users, accounts, and products. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes. Payment-specific controls could authorize a user for wire approval without permitting FX initiation.

Alongside that engineering, Matt led payments data migration, preserving client configurations, transaction histories, and compliance traceability without disruption to live payment operations. He mapped regional regulatory requirements into data visibility and access controls, sequencing rollout by regulatory readiness. He also owned production incident response and cross-team root-cause analysis.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, consolidating regional systems into a unified portal serving 135,000+ clients across 180+ countries. Its payments gateway supported payment processing across 12 countries, replacing fragmented integrations and separate regional payment entry points.
