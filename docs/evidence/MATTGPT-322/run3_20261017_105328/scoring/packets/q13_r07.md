## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for payments on ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s global corporate banking portal. The bank’s regional systems had different payment formats, client configurations, entitlement structures, and compliance records. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the requirements went well beyond its out-of-box capabilities, under aggressive Wall Street delivery timelines.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He defined the onshore-offshore engagement model and spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded with Bottomline in Portsmouth, New Hampshire. He worked with the vendor, enterprise architects, security specialists, compliance teams, and bank stakeholders to determine where native platform functionality ended and custom engineering was required.

His team delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and government tax payment modules, including payment verification through Message Authentication Codes. Matt also designed the centralized gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and payment routing across 12 countries, connecting ACCESS to clearing networks and internal settlement, compliance, and reporting systems.

For corporate administrators, he replaced individual permission configuration with role-based access, bulk changes, payment-type-specific controls, and a centralized dashboard. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes. He also led payments data migration, coordinating verification with compliance teams and preserving client configurations, transaction histories, and regulatory traceability.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries. The payments migration completed with zero disruption to live payment operations. During rollout and subsequent releases, Matt owned production incident response, coordinating root-cause analysis across bank, offshore, and vendor teams while running status updates and daily leadership calls.
