## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified corporate banking portal. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment modules, security controls, and workflows well beyond its out-of-box capabilities. Regional systems also had different message formats, entitlement structures, and compliance requirements, all while live payment processing had to continue.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, working through where platform configuration ended and bespoke engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH payments for payroll, suppliers, and collections; wire payments covering Fedwire, international wires, book transfers, and drawdowns; FX/cross-border payments with embedded rate visibility; and federal tax payments. He also implemented Message Authentication Code security for payment-integrity verification, payment-type-specific permissions, and contextual help explaining field requirements directly within payment screens.

Alongside the payment engine, Matt designed the centralized gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries. Instead of retaining separate regional integrations, the gateway connected ACCESS to clearing networks and internal compliance, settlement, and reporting systems.

The surrounding work included replacing individual permission configuration with role-based profiles and bulk changes, reducing entitlement management from days to minutes. He led payments data migration without disruption to live payment operations and translated regional regulatory requirements into data-visibility and access-control configurations. Regional launches were sequenced by regulatory readiness rather than a single global deployment.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, consolidating regional systems into a portal serving 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries. Matt also owned on-call and Sev-1 incident response, including production defect triage and cross-team root-cause analysis.
