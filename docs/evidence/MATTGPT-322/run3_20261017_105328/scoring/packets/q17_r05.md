## Visitor: How did Matt build the AWS certification program?

### A

🐾 Found it!

Matt led the AWS Launchpad program at Accenture’s Atlanta Innovation Hub from July 2018 to November 2019. Traditional training at the Liquid Studio wasn’t keeping pace with rapidly evolving AWS technologies. Engineers had gaps in cloud competency, and those gaps were slowing cloud adoption. The program’s goal was to certify engineers while preparing them to lead cloud-native transformation initiatives.

Matt led Launchpad in collaboration with AWS, building structured learning paths around AWS core services and cloud-native application development. The curriculum covered Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate, among other technologies. Rather than relying on theory alone, he delivered interactive workshops and hands-on labs that immersed engineers in real-world AWS environments.

He also established cloud expert mentorship teams so engineers could learn from experienced practitioners and retain knowledge within Accenture. Group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops brought engineers and cloud experts together to work through the material, reinforce AWS best practices, and support continued learning beyond individual certification preparation.

The certification paths included AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer. Over 200 professionals earned AWS certifications, and the program accelerated adoption and the launch of cloud-native initiatives. Alongside certification preparation, engineers received hands-on practice, expert mentorship, and group coaching intended to prepare them for cloud transformation projects.

### B

🐾 Here’s how Matt built AWS Launchpad.

At Accenture’s Liquid Studio, traditional training programs couldn’t keep pace with rapidly evolving AWS technologies. Engineers had gaps in cloud competency, and cloud adoption was slowing. The Atlanta Innovation Hub needed a structured program that would help engineers earn certifications and apply cloud skills to cloud-native initiatives.

From July 2018 to November 2019, Matt led AWS Launchpad in collaboration with AWS. He designed structured learning paths and certification programs covering core services, including Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate, alongside cloud-native application development.

Instead of relying on theory alone, Matt delivered interactive workshops and hands-on labs that immersed engineers in real-world AWS environments. Practical application was part of the training, not something participants had to figure out after completing it.

He also established cloud expert mentorship teams so engineers had people to learn from as they developed their skills. Group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops gave participants additional opportunities to work through questions and share what they were learning. The program combined structured learning, practical exercises, and ongoing support rather than treating certification as a standalone exam-preparation activity.

More than 200 professionals earned AWS certifications, including AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer certifications. Engineers gained cloud competencies to lead cloud transformation projects, and the program accelerated cloud adoption and the launch of cloud-native initiatives at Accenture.
