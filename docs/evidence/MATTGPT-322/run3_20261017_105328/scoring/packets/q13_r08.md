## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified corporate banking portal. The foundation was Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform, but the bank needed payment capabilities, security controls, and approval workflows beyond its out-of-box offering. Regional payment systems had different data models and configurations, while connectivity across 12 countries involved different message formats, protocols, and regulatory requirements.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He defined the onshore-offshore engagement model and spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices. He worked directly with Bottomline, enterprise architects, security specialists, and regional compliance officers to determine where platform configuration was sufficient and where custom engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and federal tax payment modules, alongside payment verification through Message Authentication Codes. He also designed the centralized gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and routing. For corporate administrators, he replaced individual permission configuration with role-based profiles, bulk permission changes, and payment-type-specific controls, such as allowing wire approval without FX initiation. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes.

Matt also led payments data migration, preserving client configurations, entitlement structures, transaction histories, and compliance traceability without disrupting live payment operations. He coordinated jurisdiction-specific access rules and sequenced regional launches around regulatory readiness rather than a single global deployment. During production incidents, he directed root-cause analysis and ran status updates and daily leadership calls.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ corporate, financial institution, government, and municipal clients across 180+ countries. Its payments gateway connected payment processing across 12 countries, replacing fragmented regional integrations with a single entry point.
