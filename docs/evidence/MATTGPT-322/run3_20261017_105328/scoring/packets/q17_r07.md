## Visitor: How did Matt build the AWS certification program?

### A

🐾 Found it!

The AWS Launchpad program was designed to give engineers structured cloud training and practical experience, rather than relying on theory alone.

Matt led Launchpad at the Atlanta Innovation Hub from July 2018 to November 2019, working in collaboration with AWS. He designed structured learning paths and certification programs around AWS core services and cloud-native application development. The curriculum included Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate.

The program combined those learning paths with interactive workshops and hands-on labs. Engineers worked in real-world AWS environments to reinforce practical application over theory. Certification preparation sat alongside that practical work, with support for AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer certifications.

Matt also established cloud expert mentorship teams so engineers had people to learn from as they developed their cloud competencies. He organized group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops, giving participants opportunities to work through questions together and share what they were learning. The mentorship teams embedded AWS best practices and institutional knowledge beyond the individual training sessions.

More than 200 professionals earned AWS certifications through the program. Engineers gained practical cloud experience alongside their credentials, and the program accelerated cloud adoption and the launch of cloud-native initiatives at Accenture. The learning paths, labs, mentoring, and group coaching supported ongoing capability-building across teams.

### B

🐾 Here’s how Matt built AWS Launchpad.

At Accenture’s Liquid Studio, traditional training programs weren’t keeping pace with rapidly evolving AWS technologies. Engineers had gaps in cloud competency, and cloud adoption was slowing. The Atlanta Innovation Hub needed a structured program that would help engineers earn certifications and prepare them to lead cloud-native transformation initiatives.

From July 2018 to November 2019, Matt led AWS Launchpad, developed in collaboration with AWS. Rather than relying on theory alone, he built the program around structured learning paths, hands-on labs, and mentor-based training.

He designed learning paths and certification programs covering AWS core services and cloud-native application development. Topics included Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate. Interactive workshops put engineers into real-world AWS environments so they could apply what they were learning.

Matt also established cloud expert mentorship teams to embed AWS best practices and retain knowledge across the organization. He organized group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops, giving participants support beyond the training materials. The program combined a defined path toward certification with practical exercises, expert guidance, and peer learning.

More than 200 professionals earned AWS certifications, including AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer certifications. The program accelerated AWS adoption and the launch of cloud-native initiatives across Accenture.

Matt later coordinated a separate AWS training track for Accenture’s annual ARISE HBCU Conference, assembling curriculum, presenters, and keynote speakers, personally teaching cloud fundamentals, and coordinating free AWS certificate training opportunities. That program reached more than 250 students and recent graduates each year from 2020 through 2023.
