## Visitor: Why hire Matt?

### A

🐾 Here’s what Matt has built, shipped, and changed.

Matt’s work spans Execution & Delivery, Org & Working-Model Transformation, and Talent & Enablement: enterprise platforms deployed across 12 countries, troubled programs recovered, and an engineering organization built from 0 to 150+ practitioners.

At Accenture, clients were asking for more than systems integration. They needed to move from waterfall releases and feature factories to iterative, customer-centric product development. Matt built the Atlanta and Tampa Cloud Innovation Centers with full P&L accountability, hiring and developing practitioners across engineering, architecture, product, and design.

Instead of handing teams a methodology presentation, he established pairing stations, client co-location spaces, and balanced teams of product managers, designers, and engineers. He adapted Lean XP for enterprise constraints, including compliance, legacy systems, security scanning, and access controls. Teams delivered thin-slice, production-ready solutions for user feedback. Practitioners progressed through “I do, we do, you do” coaching toward independent delivery; the talent program achieved near-zero attrition in its first 2 years.

When the practices weren’t working, he addressed the failure directly. At Norfolk Southern, defects were rising despite near-complete test coverage. Matt took ownership, audited the code and tests, and found tests written after code alongside vague backlog stories. He rebuilt the backlog with testable stories and brought TDD experts into hands-on pairing and coding katas. The client stopped finding defects the dashboard could not see.

Across the CIC practice, reported results included 4X faster velocity, zero-defect deployments, and 10-person teams delivering equivalent output to traditional 20-person teams. The centers delivered 30+ product engagements across 15+ Fortune 500 clients and contributed to \$100M+ in repeatable business through delivery excellence and client relationships. Clients continued using the teams’ practices after engagements ended.
