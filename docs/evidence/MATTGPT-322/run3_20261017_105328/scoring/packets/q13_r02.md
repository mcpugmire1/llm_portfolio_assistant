## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified corporate banking portal. Its foundation, Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform, needed custom engineering for payment types, security controls, and workflows beyond its out-of-box capabilities. Regional payment systems had different data models and integrations, while entitlement changes required days of manual configuration. The broader portal served 135,000+ clients across 180+ countries.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across the US, Mumbai, and Bangalore, spending 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices. He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and tax payment modules. That included NACHA file validation, integration with the bank’s foreign exchange engine, and Message Authentication Code security so corporate users could verify payment integrity before release.

He also designed the centralized payments gateway: message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries, replacing fragmented point-to-point integrations. For administrators, he introduced bulk permission changes across users, accounts, and products from a single screen, alongside payment-type-specific controls such as wire approval without FX initiation. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes.

Rather than building separate regional platform instances, Matt mapped jurisdiction-specific requirements into data visibility, access controls, and entitlement configuration within a single global architecture. He coordinated regional launches around regulatory readiness and introduced compliance verification dashboards. He also led payments data migration and owned production incident response.

ACCESS launched in April 2011. The payments gateway gave corporate treasurers a single entry point across regions; legacy payment data migrated with zero disruption to live payment operations; and the regional compliance framework enabled launches across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas without regulatory blockers.
