## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified corporate banking portal. The bank was consolidating regional and legacy systems, but Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform needed custom engineering for its payment workflows, security controls, and client requirements. Each payment type had distinct formatting, validation, compliance, and approval requirements, with aggressive delivery timelines.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth, New Hampshire offices, managing the boundary between native platform capabilities and bank-specific development.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and federal tax payment modules. His work included NACHA file validation, foreign-exchange integration, Message Authentication Code security, and payment-type-specific permissions, such as allowing wire approval without allowing FX initiation. He also delivered the centralized payments gateway, handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries.

For administrators, Matt replaced individual permission configuration with role-based profiles and bulk changes across users, accounts, and products. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes. He led payments data migration from legacy systems, preserving client configurations, entitlements, transaction histories, and compliance traceability with zero disruption to live payment processing.

Matt also translated regional regulatory requirements into data visibility and access controls, coordinating launches across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas based on regulatory readiness. He owned on-call and critical production incident response, including cross-team root-cause analysis and leadership updates.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ clients across 180+ countries. The gateway gave corporate treasurers a single entry point for wire, ACH, and cross-border transfers across regions, replacing separate local logins and fragmented integrations.
