## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s corporate banking portal. Its regional payment systems had different data models, formats, protocols, and compliance requirements. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment capabilities well beyond its out-of-box offering, under aggressive delivery timelines.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and JP Morgan Chase resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, determining where platform configuration ended and bespoke engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and federal tax payment modules. That included NACHA file validation, integration with the bank’s foreign exchange engine, and Message Authentication Code security so corporate users could verify payment integrity before release. He also delivered the centralized payments gateway: message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries, replacing fragmented point-to-point integrations.

The custom work extended into administration and compliance. Matt introduced bulk permission changes across users, accounts, and products, replacing individual configuration. Payment-specific controls allowed a user to approve wires without permission to initiate FX payments. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes. He also mapped regional regulatory requirements into data visibility and access controls, sequencing launches by regulatory readiness rather than a single global deployment.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ corporate, financial institution, government, and municipal clients across 180+ countries. Matt led the payments data migration without disruption to live payment operations, preserving client configurations, entitlements, transaction histories, and compliance traceability. He also owned production support and severity-1 incident response for the payment engine.
