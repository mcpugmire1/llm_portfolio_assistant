## Visitor: Why hire Matt?

### A

🐾 Matt builds products, platforms, and the teams that deliver them.

His work spans Execution & Delivery, Org & Working-Model Transformation, and Talent & Enablement. He came up from software engineer to Director, delivering enterprise platforms before building Accenture’s Cloud Innovation Centers in Atlanta and Tampa.

The centers addressed a specific problem: clients wanted to move beyond waterfall delivery, feature factories, and siloed teams, but startup practices needed adapting for legacy systems, compliance, and enterprise governance. New hires also needed hands-on experience with cloud-native technologies and modern engineering practices.

Matt brought product managers, designers, and engineers into balanced teams. He combined customer interviews, hypothesis-driven design, and thin-slice, production-ready delivery with pair programming, test-driven development, and automated pipelines. Training followed an “I do, we do, you do” progression, with mentoring, coding katas, and practice learning sessions rather than concept-only instruction.

When those practices failed to take hold at Norfolk Southern, Matt did not defend the dashboard. The client was finding defects despite near-complete test coverage. He took ownership, audited the code and tests, and found tests written after the code alongside vague backlog stories. He rebuilt the backlog with testable stories and brought in experts to work beside the engineers. The client stopped finding defects the dashboard could not see.

Across the CIC practice, Matt grew the organization from 0 to 150+ practitioners, with full P&L accountability. The centers delivered 30+ product engagements across 15+ Fortune 500 clients, achieving 4X faster velocity and zero-defect deployments through TDD and automated pipelines. At JP Morgan, teams outpaced peer velocity within the first 2 sprints. Delivery excellence and returning clients contributed to \$100M+ in repeatable business by FY23.
