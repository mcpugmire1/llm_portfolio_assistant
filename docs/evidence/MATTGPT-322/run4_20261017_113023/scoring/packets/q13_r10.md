## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s corporate digital banking portal. Its existing treasury operations depended on separate regional systems, while Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform needed substantial custom engineering to meet the bank’s requirements. Corporate treasurers faced fragmented payment systems and local logins. Client administrators spent days manually configuring user permissions.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across the US, Mumbai, and Bangalore, spending 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices. He designed and delivered payment modules for ACH credits and direct debits, wires, FX/cross-border transfers, and federal tax payments. The work included NACHA file validation, payment-integrity verification through Message Authentication Codes, sanctions screening, and payment-type-specific permissions.

He also designed the centralized payments gateway, handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries. Instead of preserving fragmented point-to-point integrations, the gateway connected ACCESS to domestic and international payment rails and internal compliance, settlement, and reporting systems.

For administrators, Matt introduced role-based profiles, bulk permission changes across users, accounts, and products, and a centralized permissions dashboard. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes. For corporate treasurers, contextual help explained payment fields and requirements directly within their workflows.

Matt led the payments data migration without disruption to live payment operations, preserving client configurations, entitlements, transaction histories, and compliance traceability. He also coordinated jurisdiction-specific access and data-visibility controls, sequencing regional launches according to regulatory readiness, and owned production incident response.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, consolidating regional systems into a unified portal serving 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries. Treasurers gained a single entry point for payments across regions, alongside real-time global reporting and connected treasury functions.
