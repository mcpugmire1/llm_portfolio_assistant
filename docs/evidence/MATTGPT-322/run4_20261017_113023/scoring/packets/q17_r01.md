## Visitor: How did Matt build the AWS certification program?

### A

🐾 Found it!

At Accenture’s Liquid Studio, traditional training programs weren’t keeping pace with rapidly evolving AWS technologies. Engineers had gaps in cloud competency, and cloud adoption was slowing. Matt led AWS Launchpad at the Atlanta Innovation Hub from July 2018 to November 2019 to build deep cloud competencies and prepare engineers to lead cloud-native transformation initiatives.

He developed the program in collaboration with AWS, combining structured learning paths, hands-on labs, and mentor-based training. The curriculum covered cloud-native application development and services including Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate. Certification paths included AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer.

Rather than relying on theory alone, Matt delivered interactive workshops and labs that immersed engineers in real-world AWS environments. Engineers could apply what they were learning, with cloud expert mentorship teams reinforcing AWS best practices. He also organized group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops so learning continued beyond individual coursework.

The program paired certification preparation with practical application and ongoing access to experienced practitioners. Its goal was not just to certify engineers, but to equip teams to lead cloud transformation projects and maintain cloud knowledge across Accenture.

More than 200 professionals earned AWS certifications through the program. The work also accelerated cloud adoption and the launch of cloud-native initiatives, while establishing mentorship and knowledge-sharing practices for continued learning.

At Accenture’s Liquid Studio, traditional training programs couldn’t keep pace with rapidly evolving AWS technologies. Engineers had gaps in cloud competency, and cloud adoption was slowing. The AWS Launchpad program addressed that problem with structured learning paths, hands-on labs, and mentor-based training, rather than theory alone.

From July 2018 to November 2019, Matt led AWS Launchpad in the Atlanta Innovation Hub as Cloud Native Architecture Lead. The program was developed in collaboration with AWS, with the goal of certifying engineers and preparing teams to lead cloud-native transformation initiatives.

Matt designed learning paths and certification programs around AWS core services and cloud-native application development. The curriculum included Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate. He delivered interactive workshops and labs that immersed engineers in real-world AWS environments, reinforcing practical application over theory.

He also established cloud expert mentorship teams so engineers could learn from experienced practitioners. Group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops complemented the individual learning paths, giving participants opportunities to work through questions together and share what they learned. Certification preparation was paired with practical cloud work and ongoing mentorship, rather than treated as an isolated exam-preparation exercise.

Through AWS Launchpad, over 200 professionals earned AWS certifications, including AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer certifications. The program accelerated cloud adoption across Accenture and supported the launch of cloud-native initiatives.
