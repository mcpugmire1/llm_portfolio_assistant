## Visitor: How did Matt build the AWS certification program?

### A

🐾 Found it!

At Accenture’s Liquid Studio, traditional training programs couldn’t keep pace with rapidly evolving AWS technologies. Engineers had gaps in cloud competency, and cloud adoption was slowing. The AWS Launchpad program was designed to give engineers structured cloud training and practical experience, rather than relying on theory alone.

Matt led Launchpad at the Atlanta Innovation Hub from July 2018 to November 2019, working in collaboration with AWS. He designed structured learning paths and certification programs around AWS core services and cloud-native application development. The curriculum included Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate.

The program combined those learning paths with interactive workshops and hands-on labs. Engineers worked in real-world AWS environments to reinforce practical application over theory. Certification preparation sat alongside that practical work, with support for AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer certifications.

Matt also established cloud expert mentorship teams so engineers had people to learn from as they developed their cloud competencies. He organized group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops, giving participants opportunities to work through questions together and share what they were learning. The mentorship teams embedded AWS best practices and institutional knowledge beyond the individual training sessions.

More than 200 professionals earned AWS certifications through the program. Engineers gained practical cloud experience alongside their credentials, and the program accelerated cloud adoption and the launch of cloud-native initiatives at Accenture. The learning paths, labs, mentoring, and group coaching supported ongoing capability-building across teams.

### B

🐾 Here’s how Matt built AWS Launchpad.

At Accenture’s Liquid Studio, traditional training programs couldn’t keep pace with rapidly evolving AWS technologies. Engineers had gaps in cloud competency, and cloud adoption was slowing. The Atlanta Innovation Hub needed a structured enablement program that would help engineers earn certifications and prepare them to lead cloud-native initiatives, rather than rely on theory alone.

From July 2018 to November 2019, Matt led AWS Launchpad in collaboration with AWS. He designed structured learning paths and certification programs covering AWS core services, including Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate, alongside cloud-native application development.

The program paired those learning paths with interactive workshops and hands-on labs. Engineers worked in real-world AWS environments to reinforce practical application over theory. Certification preparation was part of the program, but so was applying the services engineers would use on cloud transformation projects.

Matt also established cloud expert mentorship teams to embed best practices and retain institutional knowledge. He organized group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops, giving participants support beyond the formal training. The combination was structured learning, practical work, and mentor-based training, rather than a stand-alone course.

More than 200 professionals earned AWS certifications, including AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer certifications. The program accelerated cloud adoption and the launch of cloud-native initiatives at Accenture, with mentorship and knowledge-sharing supporting continued learning across teams.
