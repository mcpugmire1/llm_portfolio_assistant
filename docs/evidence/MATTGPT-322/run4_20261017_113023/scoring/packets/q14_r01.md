## Visitor: What's Matt's leadership philosophy?

### A

🐾 Here’s how Matt describes his leadership philosophy.

Matt leads with empathy, clarity, and purpose. He creates a “permit to fail” learning environment and brings Product + Engineering + Design together in balanced teams.

His own phrase is “Builder’s mindset, coach’s heart.” He teaches teams to fish, rather than just fixing problems. His core values are Empathy, Authenticity, Curiosity, Integrity, and Leadership.

Matt describes his work this way: he builds what’s next, modernizes what’s not, and grows teams along the way.
