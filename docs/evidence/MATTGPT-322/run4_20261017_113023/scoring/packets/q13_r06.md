He also delivered the centralized gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and payment routing across 12 countries.

Alongside that engineering, Matt brought together enterprise architects, security specialists, regional compliance officers, Bottomline, and the development team. They translated jurisdiction-specific requirements into data visibility rules, access controls, and rollout decisions. His entitlement engine introduced bulk permission changes and role-based profiles, reducing administration from days to minutes. He also led legacy payment data migration, production validation, and critical incident response, including root-cause coordination and daily leadership calls.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, consolidating regional systems into a portal serving 135,000+ clients across 180+ countries. The migration completed without disruption to live payments. Regional launches proceeded without regulatory blockers, with compliance dashboards becoming standard operational tooling.
