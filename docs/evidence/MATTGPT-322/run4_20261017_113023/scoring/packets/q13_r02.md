At JP Morgan Chase, Matt was Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation from August 2009 to June 2012. He led payments data migration while preserving client configurations, transaction histories, and compliance traceability, with zero disruption to live payment operations. He also owned on-call and critical production incident response.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ clients across 180+ countries. The gateway gave corporate treasurers a single entry point for wire, ACH, and cross-border transfers, replacing separate local logins and regional payment systems.

He also built payment verification through Message Authentication Codes and payment-type-specific permissions, such as allowing wire approval without allowing FX initiation. For the global payments gateway, he designed message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries, connecting ACCESS to clearing networks and internal compliance, settlement, and reporting systems.

Rather than retain individual permission configuration, Matt introduced role-based profiles and bulk changes across users, accounts, and products. Entitlement management fell from days to minutes. He also led legacy payment-data migration without disruption to live payment operations, preserving client configurations, transaction histories, and compliance traceability.

For regional rollout, Matt translated local regulatory requirements into data-visibility and access controls, sequencing launches by regulatory readiness rather than using a single global deployment. He also owned production incident response and cross-team root-cause analysis.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, consolidating regional systems into a single portal serving 135,000+ corporate, financial institution, government, and municipal clients across 180+ countries.
