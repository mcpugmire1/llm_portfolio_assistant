## Visitor: Why hire Matt?

🐾 Here’s what Matt has built, delivered, and left in place.

He came up from software engineer to Director, building enterprise platforms and cloud-native systems before building the organization that delivered them. His current search is for a role where building the product engineering organization, establishing the culture, and delivering results are part of the same job.

He also built an “I do, we do, you do” learning model, paired junior hires with experienced mentors, and established career-development frameworks. The centers achieved near-zero attrition in their first 2 years. Across the CIC practice, delivery excellence and customer relationships contributed to \$100M+ in repeat business, with 30+ product engagements across 15+ Fortune 500 clients.
