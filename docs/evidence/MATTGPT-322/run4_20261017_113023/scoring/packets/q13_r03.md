## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt was Technology Delivery Lead for payments capabilities within ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s global corporate banking portal. The bank’s regional systems had different payment formats, client configurations, entitlement structures, and compliance requirements. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but its out-of-box capabilities did not cover the requirements of 135,000+ clients across 180+ countries.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth, New Hampshire offices, determining where native platform capabilities ended and custom engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH credits and direct debits, wires, FX/cross-border payments, and federal tax payments. That included NACHA file validation, foreign exchange engine integration, and Message Authentication Code security so corporate users could validate payment integrity before release. He also delivered the centralized payments gateway, handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries.

The work extended into access controls, compliance, and production operations. Matt built payment-type-specific permissions, such as authorizing wire approval without allowing FX initiation. Bulk permission changes and role-based profiles reduced entitlement management from days to minutes. He mapped regional regulatory requirements into data visibility and access controls, sequenced launches by regulatory readiness, and owned on-call and critical production incident response.

ACCESS launched in April 2011. The payments gateway gave corporate treasurers a single entry point across regions, replacing fragmented integrations and separate local logins. Legacy client configurations, entitlements, and transaction histories were migrated without disruption to live payment operations, and the regional compliance framework enabled rollout across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas without regulatory blockers.

Alongside the payment engine, Matt designed the centralized gateway handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries. Corporate treasurers gained a single entry point for wire, ACH, and cross-border transfers rather than separate regional logins.

The supporting work included replacing individual permission configuration with role-based profiles and bulk changes, reducing entitlement management from days to minutes. Matt also led payments data migration without disruption to live payment operations, preserving client configurations, entitlements, transaction histories, and compliance traceability. For the regional rollout, he translated local regulatory requirements into data visibility and access controls, sequencing launches by regulatory readiness.
