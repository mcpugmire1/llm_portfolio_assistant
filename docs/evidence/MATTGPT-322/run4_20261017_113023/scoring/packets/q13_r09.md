## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work.

At JP Morgan Chase, Matt was Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation from August 2009 to June 2012, managing a blended Accenture and bank team of 40+ across the US, Mumbai, and Bangalore. The bank was consolidating regional treasury systems into a global portal, but Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform did not provide all the payment, security, and workflow capabilities its clients required. Regional systems also had different data models, entitlement structures, and compliance records.

Matt managed the build-vs-configure boundary: determining where WebSeries’ native capabilities ended and bank-specific engineering was required. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices. His team delivered ACH credits and direct debits, Fedwire and international wires, FX/cross-border payments, and federal tax payments, with payment-specific validation, cut-off rules, and compliance requirements. He also designed the Message Authentication Code security layer for verifying payment integrity before release.

For global connectivity, Matt designed the centralized gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries, replacing fragmented point-to-point integrations. For administrators, he replaced individual permission configuration with role-based profiles and bulk changes across users, accounts, and products. Entitlement management fell from days to minutes, while controls could distinguish wire approval from FX initiation.

Matt also led payments data migration, preserving client configurations, transaction histories, and compliance traceability with zero disruption to live payment operations. He translated regional regulatory requirements into data-visibility and access controls, sequencing launches by regulatory readiness rather than a single global deployment. He owned production incident response and cross-team root-cause analysis.

ACCESS launched in April 2011 as the bank’s unified global corporate banking portal, serving 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries. The payments gateway gave corporate treasurers a single entry point for wire, ACH, and cross-border transfers across regions.
