## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified corporate banking portal. Its treasury services ran through separate regional and legacy systems, while Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform needed custom engineering to support the bank’s payment, security, and workflow requirements. The broader portal served 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and JP Morgan Chase resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the United States. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, managing the boundary between native WebSeries capabilities and bank-specific development.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and tax payment modules. That included NACHA file validation, connectivity to the bank’s foreign exchange engine, and Message Authentication Code security so corporate users could verify payment integrity before release. He also designed the centralized payments gateway, handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries, with connections to internal compliance, settlement, and reporting systems.

For administrators, Matt replaced individual permission configuration with role-based profiles and bulk changes across users, accounts, and products. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes. He also led payments data migration without disruption to live payment operations and coordinated jurisdiction-specific data visibility and access controls across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas.

ACCESS and its Integrated Global Payments Interface launched in April 2011. Corporate treasurers gained a single entry point for wire, ACH, and cross-border transfers across regional accounts. Matt also owned production incident response, coordinating root-cause analysis across the bank, offshore engineers, and Bottomline while live payment issues were being resolved.
