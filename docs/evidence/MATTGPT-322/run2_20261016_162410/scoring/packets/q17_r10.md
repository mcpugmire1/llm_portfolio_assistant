## Visitor: How did Matt build the AWS certification program?

### A

🐾 Here’s how Matt built AWS Launchpad.

At Accenture’s Liquid Studio, traditional training programs couldn’t keep pace with rapidly evolving AWS technologies. Engineers had gaps in cloud competency, and cloud adoption was slowing. The Atlanta Innovation Hub needed a structured enablement program that would prepare engineers for cloud-native initiatives, not just certification exams.

From July 2018 through November 2019, Matt led AWS Launchpad in collaboration with AWS. He designed structured learning paths and certification programs covering AWS core services and cloud-native application development. Topics included Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate, among others. The learning paths supported certifications including AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer.

Instead of relying on theory, Matt delivered interactive workshops and hands-on labs that immersed engineers in real-world AWS environments. He established cloud expert mentorship teams to embed best practices and retain institutional knowledge. He also organized group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops, giving participants opportunities to work through questions with mentors and peers. The program combined structured study with practical application and ongoing coaching rather than treating certification preparation as a standalone course.

More than 200 professionals earned AWS certifications through the program. AWS Launchpad accelerated cloud adoption and the launch of cloud-native initiatives at Accenture, while establishing mentorship and knowledge-sharing practices for continued learning.
