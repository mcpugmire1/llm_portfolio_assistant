## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s unified global corporate banking portal. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment modules, security controls, and workflows well beyond its out-of-box capabilities. Regional systems also had different message formats, entitlement structures, and compliance requirements, all while live payment operations had to continue.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, determining where native platform capabilities ended and bespoke engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and federal tax payment modules, including NACHA file validation, foreign-exchange integration, and Message Authentication Code security for payment verification. He also designed the centralized payments gateway, handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across 12 countries, rather than retaining fragmented point-to-point integrations.

For corporate administrators, he replaced individually configured permissions with role-based profiles and bulk changes across users, accounts, and products. Payment-specific controls could authorize someone for wire approval without allowing FX initiation. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes.

Matt also led payments data migration, preserving client configurations, transaction histories, and compliance traceability without disruption to live payment operations. For regional rollout, he translated local regulatory requirements into data visibility and access controls, sequencing launches by regulatory readiness rather than using a single global deployment. He owned production incident response and cross-team root-cause analysis throughout releases and migrations.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across 180+ countries. The gateway provided a single entry point for regional payments, while jurisdiction-specific configuration enabled rollout across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas without regulatory blockers.
