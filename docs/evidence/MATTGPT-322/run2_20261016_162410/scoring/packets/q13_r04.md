## Visitor: Tell me about Matt's payments work at JPMorgan

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s payments work at JP Morgan Chase.

From August 2009 to June 2012, Matt served as Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, the bank’s global corporate banking portal. Its regional payment systems had different data models, client configurations, entitlements, and compliance requirements. Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform provided the foundation, but the bank needed payment capabilities well beyond its out-of-box offering, under aggressive delivery timelines.

Matt managed a blended team of 40+ Accenture and bank resources across Mumbai, Bangalore, and the US. He spent 70 to 80% of his time embedded at Bottomline’s Portsmouth offices, working through where native platform capabilities ended and bespoke engineering was required.

He designed and delivered ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and federal tax payment modules, including NACHA file validation, foreign exchange integration, and Message Authentication Code security for verifying payment integrity before release. He also designed the centralized gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and payment routing across 12 countries, replacing fragmented point-to-point integrations.

The work included payment-type-specific permissions, such as allowing wire approval without FX initiation. Rather than configuring permissions individually, Matt introduced role-based profiles and bulk changes across users, accounts, and products from a single screen. Entitlement management went from days to minutes.

For migration, he coordinated data verification with compliance teams, preserved client configurations and transaction histories, and ran production validation and post-cutover defect triage. For regional rollout, he translated local regulatory requirements into data visibility and access controls, sequencing launches by regulatory readiness rather than using a single global deployment. He also owned production incident response and cross-team root-cause analysis.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ corporate, financial institution, government, and municipal clients across 180+ countries. The legacy migration completed without disruption to live payment operations, and the regional compliance framework enabled launches across APAC, EMEA, and the Americas without regulatory blockers.
