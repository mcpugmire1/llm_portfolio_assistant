## Visitor: How did Matt build the AWS certification program?

### A

🐾 Here’s how Matt built AWS Launchpad.

At Accenture’s Liquid Studio, traditional training programs couldn’t keep pace with rapidly evolving AWS technologies. Gaps in cloud competency were slowing adoption, while engineers needed practical experience to lead cloud-native transformation initiatives. The program needed to build cloud competencies across Accenture, not just prepare people for an exam.

As Cloud Native Architecture Lead, Matt led AWS Launchpad at the Atlanta Innovation Hub from July 2018 to November 2019. He developed the program in collaboration with AWS, combining structured learning paths, hands-on labs, and mentor-based training.

He designed learning paths and certification programs around AWS core services, including Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, Kubernetes, and ECS/Fargate, alongside cloud-native application development. Instead of relying on theory alone, he delivered interactive workshops and labs that immersed engineers in real-world AWS environments.

Matt also established cloud expert mentorship teams to embed best practices and build institutional knowledge. Group coaching sessions and knowledge-sharing workshops gave participants additional support as they worked through the material. The structure paired certification preparation with practical application and ongoing access to experienced cloud practitioners.

More than 200 professionals earned AWS certifications, including AWS Solutions Architect and DevOps Engineer certifications. The program accelerated cloud adoption and the launch of cloud-native initiatives across Accenture. Its scope extended beyond the Atlanta training setting: the goal was to equip engineers across the organization to lead cloud transformation projects, with mentorship and shared learning continuing beyond individual certification exams.
