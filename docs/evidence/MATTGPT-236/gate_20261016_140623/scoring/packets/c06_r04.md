## Turn 1. Visitor: Has he worked in healthcare?

### A

🐾 Yes. Matt worked with a Major U.S. Health System as a Director at Accenture from May to September 2023, on AI-powered chronic disease management and responsible AI governance.

The provider’s existing condition management application supported diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease, but care was reactive, with limited real-time patient insights. Matt led an initiative to add AI-powered capabilities, starting with diabetes management. His team delivered a working MVP in 16 weeks using iterative product slicing, building an extensible foundation rather than a throwaway proof of concept.

The work included a data lake, machine learning pipeline, LLM-driven chat, and mobile delivery. The platform processed data from electronic medical records, glucometers, activity trackers, and other devices. Patients in the pilot received real-time health insights, medication reminders, and personalized daily recommendations. Matt also trained healthcare teams to interpret AI-generated insights and incorporate them into treatment plans.

Alongside delivery, the CIO raised concerns about HIPAA compliance and patient privacy. Matt brought together legal, compliance, IT security, data science, and clinical representatives to establish responsible AI practices. He operationalized Accenture’s Responsible AI Framework through anonymization, encryption, differential privacy, data-access policies, and continuous monitoring and auditing.

The pilot maintained full HIPAA compliance. Clinical teams adopted AI-generated insights into treatment planning, and the executive team approved consideration of broader AI implementation.
