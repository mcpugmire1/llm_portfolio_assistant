🐾 Matt has worked on commerce technology, though his documented experience is not a direct engagement for a retailer.

Matt joined as a developer and grew into the lead, leading 4 developers while continuing to write production code and taking ownership of the architecture and data layer.

The commerce, personalization, content, and administrative capabilities shipped as an integrated portal. Sparkfly later evolved into the retail technology platform it is today, but Matt’s work was on its earlier employee-discount and commerce platform.
