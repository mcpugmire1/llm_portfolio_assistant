### B

🐾 Matt has worked on commerce technology at Sparkfly, though that engagement was in Technology & Software rather than directly with a retailer.

At Wellfound Technology, from late 2000 through 2001, Matt helped build Sparkfly’s employee-discount portal alongside its founder. Customers were arriving faster than the platform could carry them, and the original JSP-only architecture was becoming fragile and slow. He joined as a developer and grew into the lead, managing 4 developers while continuing to write production code.

Matt built the commerce path end to end: catalog search and genre browse, product details with pricing and savings calculations, cross-sell to related titles and authors, a cart handling multiple item types, and checkout. He also built the Books, Music, and Video channels, with search by attribute, browse taxonomies, and editorially curated promotional slots. His work included account management, stored payment, personalization, administrative messaging, and the Oracle schema and PL/SQL underneath the portal.

He learned the MVC pattern and refactored the live product onto servlets and beans. The commerce, personalization, content, and administrative capabilities shipped as an integrated portal. The foundation outlasted the dot-com collapse; Sparkfly later spun off the original discount operation and evolved into the retail technology platform it is today. Matt’s work was on that earlier platform, not its later retail offering.
