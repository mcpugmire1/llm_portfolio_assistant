## Turn 1. Visitor: Has he worked in healthcare?

### A

🐾 Yes. Matt worked with a Major U.S. Health System as an Accenture Director from May to September 2023, on AI-powered chronic disease management and responsible AI governance.

The health system had an existing condition management application supporting diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease, but care was reactive and real-time patient insights were limited. Matt led an initiative to add AI-powered capabilities, starting with diabetes management. He designed and implemented a data lake, machine learning pipeline, LLM instance, and full-stack components to process data from electronic medical records, glucometers, activity trackers, and other devices.

His team integrated generative AI into a mobile app for personalized daily recommendations, medication reminders, lifestyle adjustments, and condition-specific guidance. Matt also trained healthcare teams to interpret AI-generated insights and incorporate them into treatment plans. The team delivered a working MVP in 16 weeks, built as an extensible foundation rather than a throwaway proof of concept. During the 4-month pilot, patients received real-time health insights and recommendations through the app, and clinical teams adopted AI-generated insights into treatment planning.

The CIO had also raised concerns about HIPAA compliance and patient privacy. Matt brought together legal, compliance, IT security, data science, and clinical representatives. He operationalized Accenture’s Responsible AI Framework with anonymization, encryption, differential privacy, and continuous monitoring and auditing. The pilot maintained full HIPAA compliance, and the executive team approved consideration of broader AI implementation.
