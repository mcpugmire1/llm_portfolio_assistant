## Turn 1. Visitor: Has he worked in healthcare?

### A

🐾 Yes, Matt has worked in healthcare.

As a Director at Accenture, Matt worked with a Major U.S. Health System from May to September 2023 on AI-powered chronic disease management and responsible AI governance.

The health system already had a condition management application supporting diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease, but care was reactive, with limited real-time patient insights. Starting with diabetes management, Matt led the delivery of AI-powered capabilities within the existing application. He used iterative product slicing to deliver real, extensible software rather than a throwaway proof of concept.

The work included a data lake, machine learning pipeline, LLM-driven chat, and mobile delivery. The platform ingested data from electronic medical records, glucometers, activity trackers, and other devices. Patients in the pilot received personalized daily recommendations, medication reminders, and condition-specific guidance. Matt also trained healthcare teams to interpret AI-generated insights and incorporate them into treatment plans.

Alongside delivery, the CIO raised concerns about HIPAA compliance and patient privacy. Matt brought together legal, compliance, IT security, data science, and clinical representatives. He operationalized Accenture’s Responsible AI Framework through anonymization, encryption, differential privacy, data-access policies, and continuous monitoring and auditing.

The team delivered a working MVP in 16 weeks. Clinical teams adopted AI-generated insights into treatment planning, and the pilot maintained full HIPAA compliance. The executive team approved consideration of broader AI implementation based on the governance framework’s performance during the pilot.
