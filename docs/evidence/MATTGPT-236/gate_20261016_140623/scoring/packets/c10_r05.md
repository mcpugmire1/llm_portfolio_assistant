## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about his Norfolk Southern work.

### A

🐾 Here’s what Matt worked on at Norfolk Southern.

As a Director at Accenture, Matt led Revenue and Shipment Planning modernization from November 2019 to September 2023, the Cloud Innovation Center’s first engagement. The railroad’s mainframe dated to the 1970s. Orders could fall out unnoticed until customers called, and clerks manually shepherded waybill and rating fallout through green-screen consoles. Business rules lived in COBOL and assembly maintained by a shrinking workforce. The work also faced resistance from a client organization shaped around mainframe thinking and account leadership accustomed to waterfall.

Matt led domain-driven design and event storming sessions to map business functions and dependencies. Rather than attempt a wholesale replacement, his team kept the mainframe as the system of record while building APIs, microservices, and CockroachDB alongside it. Change data capture synchronized the platforms; reconciliation checked that nothing was lost. API gateways, anti-corruption layers, Docker, Kubernetes, and automated CI/CD supported incremental replacement. The broader architecture work replaced batch reporting with real-time revenue tracking and shipment adjustments.

When the client grew restless for tangible progress, Matt brought revenue management into the innovation hub to co-create the Shipper Revenue System workspace. Weekly build-measure-learn iterations moved from personas to high-fidelity prototypes. The MVP covered 95% of targeted use cases, capturing real orders with clean data feeding the mainframe. EDI validation identified malformed data at the front door and notified trading partners, rather than waiting for customer complaints.

There was also a quality crisis: defects rose despite near-complete test coverage. Matt took ownership, audited the code and tests, and found tests written after code and stories without defined scenarios. He rebuilt the backlog with testable stories and brought in TDD experts for hands-on coaching. The client stopped finding defects the dashboard could not see.

For client teams, Matt delivered a 2-day CIC Academy course twice monthly over 6 months. Pairing rotated scarce domain expertise through the team, while contributors learned Angular, Node, Spring Boot, and cloud-native practices so they could support and manage the application after Accenture rolled off.
