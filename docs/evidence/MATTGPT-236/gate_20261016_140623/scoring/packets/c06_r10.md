## Turn 1. Visitor: Has he worked in healthcare?

### A

🐾 Yes. Matt worked with a Major U.S. Health System as a Director at Accenture from May to September 2023, on AI-powered chronic disease management and responsible AI governance.

The provider already had a condition management application supporting diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease, but care was reactive and real-time patient insights were limited. Starting with diabetes management, Matt led the initiative to add AI-powered capabilities to the existing application.

He designed and implemented a data lake, machine learning pipeline, LLM instance, and full-stack components to process data from electronic medical records, glucometers, activity trackers, and other devices. Patients in the pilot received real-time health insights and personalized daily recommendations through a mobile app, including medication reminders, lifestyle adjustments, and condition-specific guidance. Matt also trained healthcare teams to interpret AI-generated insights and incorporate them into treatment plans.

The CIO had raised concerns about HIPAA compliance and sensitive patient information. Matt brought together legal, compliance, IT security, data science, and clinical representatives. He operationalized Accenture’s Responsible AI Framework through data anonymization, encryption, differential privacy, clear data-access policies, and continuous monitoring and auditing.

The team delivered a working MVP in 16 weeks, built as an extensible foundation rather than a throwaway proof of concept. Clinical teams adopted AI-generated insights into treatment planning, and the pilot maintained full HIPAA compliance. The executive team approved consideration of broader AI implementation based on the governance framework’s performance during the pilot.
