🐾 Here’s the story of Matt’s work at Norfolk Southern.

As a Director at Accenture, Matt led the Cloud Innovation Center’s first engagement: modernizing the railroad’s Revenue and Shipment Planning platform. The mainframe dated to the 1970s and supported scheduling, billing, and order-to-cash. Orders could fall out unnoticed until customers called, business rules lived in COBOL and assembly, and clerks manually handled waybill and rating fallout. The work also faced resistance from a client organization shaped around mainframe thinking and account leadership accustomed to waterfall, while the pandemic forced remote delivery.

He brought in TDD experts, rebuilt testable stories, and reinforced rotating pairs and test-first work. A 2-day CIC Academy course ran twice monthly over 6 months, alongside hands-on learning in Angular, Node, Spring Boot, and cloud-native development.

The MVP launched covering 95% of its targeted use cases, capturing real orders with clean data feeding the mainframe. EDI validation caught fallout proactively and notified trading partners. The quality-crisis failure mode did not recur, and client contributors gained the skills to support and manage the new application after Accenture rolled off.
