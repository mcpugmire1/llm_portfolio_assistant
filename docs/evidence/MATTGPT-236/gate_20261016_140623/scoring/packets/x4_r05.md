### A

He used those velocity gains and quality improvements to address concerns about the investment in pairing. On the Fiserv recovery, the financial results were recorded separately: \$255K under budget on an \$8.5M program, \$500K in contractual penalties avoided, and a \$3M extension.

The stories do not record a specific ROI formula or percentage return. They document delivery comparisons, cost-versus-benefit analysis, and financial results, not a single calculated ROI figure.
