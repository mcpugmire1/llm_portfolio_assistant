### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s Fiserv work.

From December 2014 to June 2015, Matt served as Development Manager on an \$8.5M white-label card portal serving 2M+ cardholders across 15 financial institutions. Developers in India, testers in the Philippines, and designers in Ireland lacked visibility into work status. Requirements lived in a 200-page document, with 6-month release cycles.

Teams could catch branding and accessibility issues during sprints rather than at month 6. PMO and compliance accepted JIRA as the system of record.

Within 3 months, the team moved to 2-week sprints, and 50+ stakeholders had real-time work visibility. Matt recovered a release running 3 weeks late, avoiding \$500K in penalties. The program finished 3% under budget, saving \$255K, met all 47 acceptance criteria, and launched with zero critical defects. The portal processed \$45M in its first month with 99.97% uptime, and Fiserv awarded a \$3M contract extension.
