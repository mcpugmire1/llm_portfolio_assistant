## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about his Norfolk Southern work.

### A

🐾 Here’s the story of Matt’s work at Norfolk Southern.

As a Director at Accenture, Matt led Revenue and Shipment Planning modernization from November 2019 to September 2023, the Cloud Innovation Center’s first engagement. The railroad’s core scheduling, billing, and order-to-cash ran on a mainframe dating to the 1970s. Orders could fall out unnoticed until customers called. Business rules lived in COBOL and assembly maintained by a shrinking workforce, while billing clerks manually worked through waybill and rating fallout.

Matt led domain-driven design and event storming to map those business rules and dependencies. Rather than replace the mainframe outright, his team kept it as the system of record and built APIs, microservices, and CockroachDB alongside it. Change data capture synchronized the platforms; reconciliation checked that nothing was lost. Architecture work also included the Boris Exercise, SNAP-E documentation, and anti-corruption layers for legacy integration.

When the client grew restless for tangible progress, Matt brought revenue management into the innovation hub to co-create the Shipper Revenue System workspace through personas, prototypes, and weekly build-measure-learn iterations. He also led a 2-day CIC Academy course, delivered twice monthly over 6 months, and used rotating pairs to spread scarce legacy knowledge while client contributors learned Angular, Node, Spring Boot, and cloud-native concepts.

The engagement also faced a quality crisis: defects rose despite near-complete test coverage. Matt took ownership, audited the code and tests, and found tests written after code and backlog stories without defined scenarios. He brought in TDD experts, rebuilt testable stories, and reinforced test-first work through daily pairing, coding katas, and coaching. The failure mode did not recur after the experts left.

The MVP launched covering 95% of its targeted use cases, capturing real orders with clean data feeding the mainframe. EDI validation moved fallout detection from customer complaints to proactive trading-partner notifications. Delivery scaled to 3 balanced teams, and the client was co-creating in weekly iterations and owning its backlog and priorities.
