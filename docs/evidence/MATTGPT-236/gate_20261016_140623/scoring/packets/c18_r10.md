Matt conducted an architecture assessment and gap/fit analysis, drawing on prior experience implementing the same platform. When the previous data conversion lead left, Matt also took over conversion planning from scratch. He documented source-to-target transformation rules, planned mock conversion cycles, and established reconciliation checks for payment amounts and beneficiary details.
