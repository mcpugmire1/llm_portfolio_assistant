Matt led Norfolk Southern’s Revenue and Shipment Planning modernization as a Director at Accenture, from November 2019 to September 2023. It was the Cloud Innovation Center’s first engagement. The railroad’s mainframe dated to the 1970s: orders could fall out unnoticed until customers called, business rules lived in COBOL and assembly, and clerks manually worked through waybill and rating fallout. The client was accustomed to mainframe thinking, account leadership to waterfall delivery, and the pandemic moved the team into remote work.

Matt took ownership, audited the code and tests, and found tests written after code alongside vague backlog stories. He brought in TDD experts, rebuilt stories with defined scenarios and a definition of done, and reinforced test-first work through daily rotating pairs, coding katas, and standup checks. The failure mode did not recur after the experts left.

For client enablement, Matt ran a 2-day CIC Academy course twice monthly over 6 months. Domain experts and engineers paired through “I do, we do, you do,” spreading legacy knowledge while client contributors learned Angular, Node, Spring Boot, and cloud-native concepts.

The MVP launched covering 95% of its targeted use cases. It captured real orders with clean data feeding the mainframe, while EDI validation moved fallout detection from customer complaints to proactive trading-partner notifications. The client team was co-creating in weekly iterations and had learned to own its backlog and priorities.
