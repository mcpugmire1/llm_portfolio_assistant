## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about his Norfolk Southern work.

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s Norfolk Southern work.

From November 2019 through September 2023, Matt worked as a Director on Revenue and Shipment Planning modernization, the Cloud Innovation Center’s first engagement. Core scheduling, billing, and order-to-cash ran on a mainframe dating to the 1970s. Orders could fall out unnoticed until customers called, business rules lived in COBOL and assembly, and clerks manually handled waybill and rating fallout. The railroad had to keep running throughout modernization.

Matt led domain-driven design and event storming sessions to map business functions, data flows, and dependencies. His team built APIs, microservices, and CockroachDB alongside the mainframe, which remained the system of record. Change data capture synchronized the platforms, and reconciliation checked that nothing was lost. Architecture work also included the Boris Exercise, SNAP-E documentation, and anti-corruption layers for legacy integration.

When the client grew restless for tangible progress, Matt brought revenue management into the innovation hub to co-create the Shipper Revenue System workspace. They moved from personas and user flows to high-fidelity prototypes in weekly build-measure-learn iterations.

The engagement also hit a quality crisis: defects were rising despite near-complete test coverage. Matt took ownership, audited the code and tests, and found tests written after code and backlog stories without defined scenarios. He brought in TDD experts, rebuilt testable stories, and reinforced test-first work through rotating pairs, coding katas, and daily follow-up. The client stopped finding defects the dashboard could not see, and that failure mode did not recur.

For enablement, Matt led a 2-day CIC Academy course, delivered twice monthly over 6 months. Pairing spread scarce legacy knowledge and helped client contributors learn Angular, Node, Spring Boot, and cloud-native practices.

The MVP launched covering 95% of targeted use cases, capturing real orders with clean data feeding the mainframe. EDI validation moved fallout detection from customer complaints to proactive trading-partner notifications. Delivery scaled to 3 balanced teams, with client teams owning their backlog and co-creating in weekly iterations.
