## Turn 1. Visitor: Has he worked in healthcare?

### A

🐾 Yes. Matt worked as a Director at Accenture with a Major U.S. Health System from May to September 2023, on AI-powered chronic disease management and responsible AI governance.

The provider had an existing condition management application for diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease, but care was largely reactive, with limited real-time patient insights. Starting with diabetes management, Matt led the initiative to add AI-powered capabilities to the existing application. The CIO also raised concerns about HIPAA compliance and the risks of processing sensitive medical information.

Rather than building a throwaway proof of concept, Matt used iterative product slicing to deliver working, extensible software. His team implemented a data lake, machine learning pipeline, LLM-driven chat, and mobile components, bringing together data from electronic medical records, glucometers, activity trackers, and other devices. Patients received personalized daily recommendations, medication reminders, and condition-specific guidance. Matt also trained healthcare teams to interpret AI-generated insights and incorporate them into treatment plans.

Alongside delivery, he brought together legal, compliance, IT security, data science, and clinical representatives. He operationalized Accenture’s Responsible AI Framework through anonymization, encryption, differential privacy, data-access policies, and continuous monitoring and auditing.

The team delivered a working MVP in 16 weeks. During the 4-month pilot, patients received real-time AI-generated insights through the mobile app, clinical teams incorporated those insights into treatment planning, and full HIPAA compliance was maintained. The executive team approved consideration of broader AI implementation based on the governance framework’s performance during the pilot.
