## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about his Fiserv work.

### A

🐾 Here’s what Matt worked on at Fiserv.

From December 2014 to June 2015, Matt served as Development Manager on an \$8.5M white-label card portal serving 2M+ cardholders across 15 financial institutions. He joined roughly 7 months into the engagement, after multiple missed deadlines, poor quality, and production payment-processing defects. Client stakeholders were threatening contract cancellation. The 45-person team was globally dispersed, and 3 external vendors handling payment processing, fraud detection, and hosting had uncoordinated dependencies.

Matt traveled to Omaha Monday through Thursday and ran 7 AM and 11 PM transition calls across offshore and onshore teams. He reprioritized the backlog, reset timelines, and coordinated vendor deliverables. He also managed the integration layer connecting the VisionPLUS 350 mainframe’s COBOL payment processing to a Java EE/Spring portal through MuleSoft, Tibco, and WebMethods during the transition to an SOA-based API architecture.

The working-model change went beyond adding sprint meetings. Matt replaced a single To Do/Done JIRA board with a multi-tiered Kanban/Scrum structure, tracked 47 acceptance criteria as Epics, and decomposed a 200-page requirements document into persona-based user stories. Sprint demos brought branding, UI, and ADA reviews forward instead of leaving them until month 6. To address PMO and compliance resistance, he used JIRA audit trails, attached ADA scans, and developer comments as the system of record, while maintaining steering committee reporting.

Within 3 months, the team moved from 6-month release cycles to 2-week sprints. Matt recovered a release that was 3 weeks behind, delivering before the holiday freeze and avoiding \$500K in penalties.

The program finished 3% under budget, preserving \$255K, with 47 of 47 acceptance criteria met and zero critical defects at launch. The portal processed \$45M in transactions in its first month with 99.97% uptime, and Fiserv awarded a \$3M contract extension.
