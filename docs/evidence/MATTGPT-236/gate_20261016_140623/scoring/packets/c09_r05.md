At the Cloud Innovation Center, teams achieved 4x delivery acceleration after embracing lean product and modern engineering practices. ## Turn 3. Visitor: Give me an example.

### A

🐾 Here’s a specific example from Norfolk Southern.

In 2020, Matt’s Cloud Innovation Center team was modernizing the company’s legacy Revenue and Shipment Planning platform. A few months into the engagement, as the pandemic forced everyone into remote work, the client reported rising defects. The team’s dashboards showed near-complete test coverage. The discrepancy put the team’s work under scrutiny from both Norfolk Southern and Accenture account leadership.

Matt took ownership in front of both groups rather than defending the metrics. He led a bottom-up audit of the codebase and test suite and shared the gaps openly. An engineer had been building from his own interpretation rather than the backlog story, then writing tests after the code. The backlog was also part of the problem: stories lacked defined scenarios and a definition of done, leaving engineers without clear requirements to build and test against.

Instead of stopping at a process correction, Matt rebuilt the backlog with SMART, testable stories and brought in frontend and backend TDD experts from Columbus to work side by side with the team. They redid the code and tests, then reinforced the practices through boot camps, coding katas, practice learning sessions, and pairing on an “I do, we do, you do” progression. Product managers also received training on product management and SMART stories.

Defect rates dropped as story quality and test-first discipline took hold. Engineers had clearer requirements and hands-on coaching. Norfolk Southern stopped finding defects the dashboard could not see, and client confidence was restored through the open audit and visible improvement.
