### B

🐾 Here’s the closest match.

Matt has worked on commerce and retail-related technology through Sparkfly, rather than directly for a retailer. His Sparkfly engagement was classified as Technology & Software. The business began with an employee discount program and later evolved into the retail technology platform it is today.

From late 2000 through 2001, Matt worked on the platform at Wellfound Technology. The founder was bringing in customers faster than the platform could carry them. Matt joined as a developer and grew into the lead, taking ownership of the architecture and data layer while leading four developers and continuing to write production code.

He built the commerce path end to end: catalog search and genre browsing, product details with pricing and savings calculations, cross-selling to related titles and authors, a cart handling multiple item types, and checkout. He also built Books, Music, and Video channels, personalized content, account management, and administrative messaging. When the original JSP-only architecture became fragile and slow, he learned the MVC pattern and refactored the live product onto servlets and beans.

The commerce, personalization, content, and administrative capabilities shipped as one integrated portal. The foundation outlasted the dot-com collapse, and Sparkfly later spun off its original discount operation and evolved into retail technology. That later evolution should not be confused with Matt having worked on its present-day retail platform.
