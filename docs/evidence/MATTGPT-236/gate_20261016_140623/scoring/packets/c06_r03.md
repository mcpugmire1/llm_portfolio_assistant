## Turn 1. Visitor: Has he worked in healthcare?

### A

🐾 Yes. Matt worked with a Major U.S. Health System as a Director at Accenture from May to September 2023, on AI-powered chronic disease management and responsible AI governance.

The health system already had a condition management application supporting diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease, but care was largely reactive, with limited real-time patient insights. The initiative started with diabetes management. At the same time, the CIO raised concerns about HIPAA compliance and the risks of processing sensitive medical information.

Matt led the initiative to add a data lake, machine learning pipeline, LLM-driven chat, and proactive alerting to the existing application. The platform brought together data from electronic medical records, glucometers, activity trackers, and other devices. Patients received personalized daily recommendations through a mobile app, including medication reminders, lifestyle adjustments, and condition-specific guidance. Matt also trained healthcare teams to interpret AI-generated insights and incorporate them into treatment plans.

He delivered a working MVP in 16 weeks through iterative product slicing, building real, extensible software rather than a throwaway proof of concept. The pilot cohort received real-time, generative AI-driven health insights, a capability the organization had never delivered.

Alongside delivery, Matt brought together legal, compliance, IT security, data science, and clinical representatives. He operationalized privacy controls, monitoring, and auditing. The pilot maintained full HIPAA compliance, and its outcomes built executive confidence for broader implementation.
