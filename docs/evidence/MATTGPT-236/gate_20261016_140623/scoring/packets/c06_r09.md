## Turn 1. Visitor: Has he worked in healthcare?

### A

🐾 Yes, Matt worked with a Major U.S. Health System as an Accenture Director from May to September 2023, on AI-powered chronic disease management and responsible AI governance.

The health system had an existing condition management application supporting diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease, but care was largely reactive, with limited real-time patient insights. The initiative began with diabetes management. At the same time, the CIO raised concerns about HIPAA compliance and the risks of processing sensitive medical information.

Matt led delivery of AI-powered capabilities within the existing application. The team built a data lake, machine learning pipeline, LLM-driven chat, and mobile components to process data from electronic medical records, glucometers, activity trackers, and other devices. Patients in the pilot received real-time health insights and personalized recommendations, including medication reminders and lifestyle guidance. Matt also trained healthcare teams to interpret AI-generated insights and incorporate them into treatment plans.

For governance, he brought together representatives from legal, compliance, IT security, data science, and clinical departments. They established policies for data access, model transparency, and accountability, with anonymization, encryption, differential privacy, continuous monitoring, and auditing. Matt regularly updated the CIO and executive team and adjusted the work based on their feedback.

The team delivered a working MVP in 16 weeks, built as an extensible foundation rather than a throwaway proof of concept. The pilot maintained full HIPAA compliance, clinical teams adopted AI-generated insights into treatment planning, and the executive team approved consideration of broader AI implementation.
