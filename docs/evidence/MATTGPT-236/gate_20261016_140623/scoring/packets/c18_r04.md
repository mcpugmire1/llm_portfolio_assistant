## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about his Norfolk Southern work.

### A

🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s Norfolk Southern work.

As a Director at Accenture, Matt led Revenue and Shipment Planning modernization from November 2019 to September 2023, the Cloud Innovation Center’s first engagement. Core scheduling, billing, and order-to-cash ran on a mainframe dating to the 1970s. Orders could fall out unnoticed until customers called, business rules lived in COBOL and assembly maintained by a shrinking workforce, and clerks manually handled waybill and rating fallout. Wholesale replacement was too risky: modernization had to happen without stopping the railroad.

Matt led domain-driven design and event storming sessions to map business functions and dependencies. He used the Boris Exercise to map APIs and data flows, with SNAP-E documentation capturing dependencies and risks. Rather than a big-bang cutover, his team built APIs, microservices, and CockroachDB alongside the mainframe. Change data capture synchronized the platforms, and reconciliation checked that nothing was lost. API gateways and anti-corruption layers supported incremental replacement, while Docker, Kubernetes, and automated CI/CD pipelines supported the new services.

When the client grew restless for tangible progress, Matt brought revenue management into the innovation hub to co-create the Shipper Revenue System UI. Teams worked from personas through high-fidelity prototypes in weekly build-measure-learn iterations. Delivery scaled to three balanced teams, with one team beginning transition to Monterrey.

There were setbacks. Rising defects contradicted near-complete test coverage. Matt audited the code and tests, found tests written after code and vague backlog stories, and brought in TDD experts for hands-on coaching. He also led a two-day CIC Academy course, delivered twice a month over six months. Pairing rotated scarce domain knowledge through the team and helped client contributors learn Angular, Node, Spring Boot, and cloud-native practices.

The MVP launched covering 95% of its targeted use cases, capturing real orders with clean data feeding the mainframe. EDI validation moved fallout detection from customer complaints to proactive trading-partner notifications. Maintenance expenses declined, deployment cycles accelerated, and the client stopped finding defects the dashboard could not see.
