🐾 Here’s the story behind Matt’s Norfolk Southern work.

Matt led the Revenue and Shipment Planning modernization as a Director at Accenture, from November 2019 through September 2023. It was the Cloud Innovation Center’s first engagement. The railroad’s mainframe dated to the 1970s: orders could fall out unnoticed until customers called, business rules lived in COBOL and assembly maintained by a shrinking workforce, and clerks manually handled waybill and rating fallout through green-screen consoles. He brought in TDD experts, rebuilt stories with defined scenarios, and introduced coding katas and hands-on coaching.

Enablement ran alongside delivery. Matt led a 2-day CIC Academy course twice monthly over 6 months. Thin-stretched domain experts paired with engineers using “I do, we do, you do,” rotating legacy knowledge through the team. Client contributors learned Angular, Node, Spring Boot, and cloud-native concepts to support the application themselves.

The MVP covered 95% of targeted use cases. EDI validation detected fallout proactively and notified trading partners, rather than waiting for customer calls. The client stopped finding defects the dashboard could not see and was co-creating in weekly iterations.
