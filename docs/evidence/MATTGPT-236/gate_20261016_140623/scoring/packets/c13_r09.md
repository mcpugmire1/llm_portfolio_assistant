He wrote the portal’s ADA manifesto and built screen-reader audits into the Definition of Done. Fiserv adopted those accessibility standards for later releases.

Delivery stabilized within 3 months. The program finished 3% under budget, saving \$255K, with all 47 acceptance criteria met and zero critical defects at launch. The portal processed \$45M in its first month at 99.97% uptime. Matt also recovered a release running 3 weeks late, avoiding \$500K in penalties, and secured a \$3M contract extension.
