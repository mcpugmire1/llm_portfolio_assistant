## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about his Fiserv work.

### A

🐾 Here’s what Matt worked on at Fiserv.

From December 2014 through June 2015, Matt served as Development Manager on Fiserv’s \$8.5M white-label card portal, the shared platform behind branded credit card sites for 2M+ cardholders across 15 financial institutions. He joined roughly 7 months into the engagement, after multiple missed deadlines, poor quality, and production payment defects had put contract cancellation on the table. The 45-person globally dispersed team lacked visibility into work, while 3 external vendors had uncoordinated dependencies across payment processing, fraud detection, and hosting.

Matt traveled Monday through Thursday to Omaha and ran 7 AM and 11 PM transition calls across offshore and onshore teams. He reprioritized the backlog, reset timelines, and coordinated vendor deliverables. The technical work included an SOA integration layer wrapping the VisionPLUS 350 mainframe’s COBOL functionality into a Java EE/Spring portal through MuleSoft, Tibco, and WebMethods.

He also changed how the team worked. Instead of 6-month waterfall release cycles and a 200-page requirements document organized by system modules, he introduced 2-week sprints and persona-based user stories. He restructured JIRA to track the 47 acceptance criteria as epics and introduced sprint demos to catch branding, UI, and ADA compliance issues early. PMO and compliance accepted JIRA as the system of record, including audit trails and attached accessibility scans.

Delivery stabilized within 3 months. Matt recovered a release that was 3 weeks behind schedule before the holiday freeze, avoiding \$500K in penalties. The program finished 3% under budget, saving \$255K, with 47 of 47 acceptance criteria met and zero critical defects at launch. The portal processed \$45M in transactions in its first month with 99.97% uptime, and Fiserv awarded a \$3M contract extension.
