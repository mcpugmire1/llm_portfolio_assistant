Rather than separate regional platforms or a single big-bang rollout, he implemented jurisdiction-specific configuration and sequenced launches by regulatory readiness.

ACCESS launched in April 2011, serving 135,000+ clients across 180+ countries. Entitlement administration fell from days to minutes, legacy migration caused no disruption to live payments, and regional launches proceeded without regulatory blockers.
