Matt led the Revenue and Shipment Planning modernization as a Director at Accenture, from November 2019 through September 2023. From June 2012 to June 2013, Matt served as a Senior Technology Architecture Manager at Accenture on RBC’s commercial banking modernization. The bank’s existing corporate banking systems had performance and scalability limitations and lacked digital offerings. RBC was evaluating Bottomline WebSeries as the payment hub engine for its Day2Day Corporate Banking platform, replacing the aging RBC Express portal. Leadership needed to know whether the proposed platform could meet the business requirements before committing further investment.

Matt conducted an architecture assessment and gap/fit analysis, drawing on prior experience implementing WebSeries on a large-scale payments program. He reviewed code, interviewed stakeholders, analyzed documentation, and assessed Data, Application, Technology, and Business Architecture. He created architectural heat maps showing where the proposed platform fell short, then worked with RBC’s enterprise architecture team on logical and system models and a transparent cost-to-fix versus benefit comparison. Workshops with bank leadership brought the technical findings into discussions of business goals.

He also established the program management office, coordinating RBC leadership, Accenture teams, and Bottomline Technologies. He defined staffing, responsibilities, budgets, schedules, and communications. Designated contacts, shared real-time updates, daily summaries, and a task-tracking matrix gave teams a common view of assignments and dependencies. When inputs were missing, he followed up with contributors and escalated to department leads.

The assessment found that WebSeries lacked the modularity required for RBC’s long-term digital operating model. The bank avoided further investment in that platform path and redirected technical resources to higher-priority initiatives. The PMO improved coordination across 3 organizations, eliminated missed program updates, and reduced redundant effort. The heat maps and roadmap artifacts remained reference material for future modernization planning.
