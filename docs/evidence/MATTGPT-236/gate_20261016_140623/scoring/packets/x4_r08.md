🐾 Here’s what Matt measured.

Matt used cost-to-fix versus benefit comparisons, engagement financials, and delivery results to make investment cases. The stories do not specify a single ROI formula, a payback period, or a calculated ROI percentage.

Matt conducted code reviews, stakeholder interviews, and a gap/fit analysis. Those are delivery comparisons, not a documented financial ROI percentage.

Matt managed actuals, forecast variance, contingency release, staffing costs, and scope changes. The story reports those amounts separately, rather than combining preserved margin, avoided penalties, and extension revenue into a single ROI figure.
