The Fiserv program finished 3% under budget, met 47/47 acceptance criteria, launched with zero critical defects, and secured a \$3M contract extension. JP Morgan ACCESS launched in April 2011; its gateway supported payments across 12 countries, while entitlement administration dropped from days to minutes. The distinction was program recovery and working-model change at Fiserv, versus payment-platform engineering and access governance at JP Morgan ACCESS.
