## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about a time he failed.

### A

🐾 Here's a story about a challenging experience Matt faced.

While leading a global platform modernization delivery for a **Fortune 500 financial services client**, Matt encountered a significant failure due to assumptions made about the infrastructure. The project involved multiple environments, including dev, QA, UAT, and production, and was under intense delivery pressure. Matt assumed that the IAM controls, environment boundaries, and data masking were correctly configured based on inherited documentation. This assumption led to an offshore tester accidentally triggering an email job in UAT, which sent communications containing unmasked production data to thousands of real clients.

Matt took full accountability for the incident, leading the team through a root cause analysis and remediation process. He learned that assumptions are risks in disguise and emphasized the importance of validating IAM configurations, environment verifications, PII scrubbing, and risk ownership from day one. This experience reinforced the need for leaders to own the environments their teams operate in, ensuring that such oversights do not occur again.
