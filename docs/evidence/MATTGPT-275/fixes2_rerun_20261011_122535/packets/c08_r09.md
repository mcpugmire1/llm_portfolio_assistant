## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about a time he failed.

### A

🐾 Here's a story about a challenging experience Matt faced.

During a global platform modernization delivery for a **Fortune 500 financial services client**, Matt encountered a significant failure. He was the tech lead responsible for ensuring a safe environment setup across multiple environments: dev, QA, UAT, and production. Under delivery pressure, he assumed that the inherited IAM controls, environment boundaries, and downstream access configurations were correct. This assumption led to an offshore tester accidentally triggering an email job in UAT, which sent communications containing unmasked production customer data to thousands of real clients.

Matt took full accountability for the incident and led the team through a root cause analysis and remediation process. The root cause was identified as insufficient IAM checks, unclear environment boundaries, and production data that had not been scrubbed from downstream environments. Unfortunately, this systemic failure resulted in the client demanding the removal of the tester from the project. This incident reinforced Matt's belief in the importance of leaders owning the environment their teams operate in and highlighted that assumptions are risks in disguise. As a result, IAM validation, environment verification, PII scrubbing, and risk ownership became day-one leadership responsibilities in his delivery approach.
