## Turn 2. Visitor: Give me an example where that didn’t work.

### A

🐾 Here's a story where Matt faced a leadership challenge.

While leading a global platform modernization delivery for a **Fortune 500 financial services client**, Matt encountered a significant issue due to assumptions he made. He assumed the IAM controls, environment boundaries, and data masking were correctly set up. This oversight led to an offshore tester accidentally triggering an email job in UAT, sending unmasked production data to thousands of real clients. The root cause was insufficient IAM checks and unclear environment boundaries, which were inherited without verification.

Matt took accountability for the incident and led the team through root cause analysis and remediation. Despite his efforts, the client demanded the removal of the tester from the project. This incident reinforced Matt's belief that leaders must own the environment their teams operate in. It taught him that assumptions are risks in disguise and that IAM validation, environment verification, PII scrubbing, and risk ownership must be day-one leadership responsibilities.
