## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about a time he failed.

### A

🐾 Here's a story where things didn't go as planned.

This experience reinforced his belief that leaders must own the environment their teams operate in and ensure that all configurations are verified rather than assumed.

The failure led to significant consequences, including the client's demand to remove the tester from the project, underscoring the systemic nature of the failure and its impact on individuals.
