Matt took accountability for the incident and led the team through root cause analysis and remediation.

This experience taught him that assumptions are risks in disguise and reinforced his belief that leaders own the environment their teams operate in. As a result, he now prioritizes IAM validation, environment verification, PII scrubbing, and risk ownership as day-one leadership responsibilities in his delivery approach.
