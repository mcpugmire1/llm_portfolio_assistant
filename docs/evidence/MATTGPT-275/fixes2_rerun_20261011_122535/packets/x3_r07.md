## Turn 1. Visitor: Has Matt worked on trading platforms?

### A

🐾 Matt has worked on trading platforms, particularly during his time at **Cendian Chemical Logistics**. He was involved in designing and delivering integrations for Cendian's trading network, which coordinated shipments across a vast network of more than **300 carriers** into and out of **80-plus countries**. This involved configuring trading profiles and exchange documents in webMethods B2B Trading Networks, which served as the platform for B2B document exchange with trading partners.

Matt engineered integrations using Java and webMethods Integration Server for various transaction sets, developed common data representation XML standards based on CIDX, and created canonical business objects to normalize different formats into a single shape. He also built portal capabilities for partners who could not integrate by code and brought document generation in-house, enhancing the efficiency and capability of the trading network.

These efforts ensured that partners across the full range of capabilities could exchange with Cendian, whether through EDI, XML services, or a portal for those unable to integrate directly, thereby enhancing the trading platform's functionality and reach.
