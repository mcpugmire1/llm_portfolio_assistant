### B

🐾 Here's a great example.

Matt worked on the **JP Morgan ACCESS** platform, which was a comprehensive global payments solution. As the Technology Delivery Lead, he managed a team of over **40** across multiple locations, including the US, Mumbai, and Bangalore. The project involved building bespoke payment modules, security layers, and platform capabilities on top of Bottomline Technologies' WebSeries platform. This was not a standard deployment; the ACCESS platform served **135,000+** corporations, financial institutions, governments, and municipalities across **180+ countries**.

The work included designing and delivering payment modules for ACH, wire, FX/cross-border, and EFTPS tax payments, along with implementing a Message Authentication Code (MAC) security layer and real-time global reporting. The platform also integrated with JP Morgan's centralized transaction monitoring system, which screened trillions of daily transactions for suspicious activity.

The ACCESS platform launched in April 2011 as JP Morgan's unified global corporate banking portal and has been recognized as #1 in digital channels by Coalition Greenwich. This engagement spanned from August 2009 to June 2012.
