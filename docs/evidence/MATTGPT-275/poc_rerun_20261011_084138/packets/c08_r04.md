## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about a time he failed.

### A

🐾 Here's a story about a challenging situation Matt faced.

During a global platform modernization delivery for a **Fortune 500 financial services client**, Matt encountered a significant issue due to assumptions he made about the existing infrastructure. He was responsible for ensuring a safe environment setup across multiple environments: development, QA, UAT, and production. However, he relied on inherited documentation and assumed that IAM controls, environment boundaries, and data masking were correctly configured. This oversight led to an offshore tester accidentally triggering an email job in UAT, which sent communications containing unmasked production data to thousands of real clients.

Matt took accountability for the incident and led the team through a root cause analysis and remediation process. He learned that assumptions are risks in disguise and that IAM validation, environment verification, PII scrubbing, and risk ownership should be day-one leadership responsibilities. The incident resulted in the client demanding the removal of the tester from the project, highlighting the systemic failure and reinforcing Matt's belief in the importance of leaders owning the environment their teams operate in.
