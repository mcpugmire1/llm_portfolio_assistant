Additionally, he coached offshore teams on empowerment and trained them on screen reader audits as part of the Definition of Done.

The result was a successful transition to agile, providing real-time visibility to over **50** stakeholders across three time zones. The offshore teams adopted an empowerment model, integrating screen reader audits into their workflow.
