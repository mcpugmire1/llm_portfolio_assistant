🐾 Here’s the distinction: Matt has worked on global payments, FX integration, trade finance, and B2B trading networks. His stories do not establish direct work on securities trading or exchange execution platforms.

At **JP Morgan Chase**, Matt was Technology Delivery Lead for ACCESS Next Generation, a global corporate banking portal. The bank needed payment capabilities beyond Bottomline Technologies’ WebSeries platform, with distinct formatting, validation, compliance, and workflow requirements for each payment type.

Matt managed a blended team of **40+** and built the FX/cross-border payment module integrating with the bank’s foreign exchange engine. Corporate treasurers could initiate cross-currency payments within ACCESS, with embedded rate visibility and connectivity to international clearing systems. He also architected single sign-on connecting cash, trade, and investment operations, and drove the groundwork for trade finance integration so users could view a trade transaction’s lifecycle from initiation to final payment. These were treasury and payment capabilities—not documented securities order execution work.

He also designed the centralized payments gateway, handling message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across ACH, wire, FX, and multi-currency channels in **12 countries**. It launched in April **2011**, replacing fragmented regional integrations. His related work included legacy payment migration without disruption to live operations and jurisdiction-specific compliance configuration.

At **Cendian Chemical Logistics**, Matt worked on webMethods B2B Trading Networks. Here, “trading” meant exchanging orders, shipment data, and invoices with logistics partners—not financial instruments. He built CIDX-based document mappings and canonical business objects, and onboarded a plastics client through end-to-end testing and production validation against live shipment planning.
