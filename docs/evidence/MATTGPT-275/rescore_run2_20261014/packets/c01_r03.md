## Turn 3. Visitor: What went wrong?

### A

🐾 The specific production failure was in the payment-verification security layer at **JP Morgan Chase**.

The Message Authentication Code (MAC) layer let corporate users validate payment integrity before release. A production failure in that layer was traced to **missing unit tests and weak error handling**. Matt had break-glass production access and queried live payment data through SQL to triage the defect. The account does not specify the failure’s duration, affected payment volume, or whether customers lost money.

There were also post-cutover production defects, primarily **data issues carried over from the legacy platform during migration**. The regional systems had different schemas, client configurations, entitlement structures, and compliance records. Matt ran UAT and production validation/shakeout testing, coordinated data verification with compliance teams, and triaged the defects that emerged after cutover. The migration nevertheless completed **without disruption to live payment operations**.

The surrounding operational problems predated ACCESS. Payment connectivity across **12 countries** relied on fragmented integrations with different formats, protocols, and regulatory requirements. User permissions had accumulated through individual configuration over years, leaving administrators with poor visibility into who could access which payment functions. Entitlement changes took days of manual work.

Matt delivered a centralized payments gateway and introduced bulk permission changes across users, accounts, and products from a single screen—a capability absent from the legacy systems. Entitlement management dropped from days to minutes.

During live production issues, Matt owned on-call and severity-**1** incident response, coordinated root-cause analysis across the bank, offshore teams, and Bottomline Technologies, and ran status updates and daily leadership calls. The accounts document those failures and responses, but not a detailed incident-by-incident resolution timeline.
