### B

🐾 Here's a great example.

In one of his roles at **Accenture**, Matt was responsible for managing the financial aspects of various engagements, including those at **JP Morgan**, **RBC**, **Fiserv**, and **HSBC**. His approach to calculating ROI involved a meticulous focus on engagement margin and cost management. He managed the resource pyramid by staffing senior and specialized resources early, then transitioning to offshore and nearshore teams as the work became more routine. This approach helped in optimizing costs and maintaining a healthy margin.

Matt also pushed scope changes into formal change requests rather than absorbing them, ensuring that any additional work was compensated. He managed contingency releases, forecast variances, and conducted deep-dive reviews under financial scrutiny. For example, in a program at **Fiserv**, he recovered an **\$8.5M** project to **3%** under budget, avoided **\$500K** in penalties, and secured a **\$3M** extension. These actions directly impacted the ROI by preserving margin and converting potential losses into profitable extensions.

At a broader level, as a Director running two Cloud Innovation Centers, Matt focused on minimizing unassigned and unbillable downtime, enforcing internal bench deployment before approving third-party contractors, and converting contractors to internal hires when roles became permanent. This disciplined approach to financial management helped maintain a strong ROI across his engagements.
