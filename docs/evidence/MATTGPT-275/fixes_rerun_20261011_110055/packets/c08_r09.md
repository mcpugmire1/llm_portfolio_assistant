## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about a time he failed.

### A

🐾 Here's a story about a significant challenge Matt faced.

During a global platform modernization project for a **Fortune 500 financial services client**, Matt encountered a failure due to assumptions he made about the existing infrastructure. The project involved multiple environments, including development, QA, UAT, and production, with offshore teams executing test cycles. Under delivery pressure, Matt assumed that the IAM controls, environment boundaries, and data masking were correctly set up based on inherited documentation from prior phases.

Unfortunately, these assumptions led to a critical incident. An offshore tester accidentally triggered an email job in the UAT environment, which sent communications containing unmasked production data to thousands of real clients. This incident highlighted the lack of proper IAM checks, unclear environment boundaries, and the absence of data scrubbing in downstream environments.

Matt took accountability for the incident and led the team through a thorough root cause analysis and remediation process. He learned that assumptions are risks in disguise and emphasized the importance of validating IAM configurations, verifying environment setups, and ensuring data masking. This experience reinforced his belief that leaders must own the environments their teams operate in, and he integrated these lessons into his delivery approach going forward.
