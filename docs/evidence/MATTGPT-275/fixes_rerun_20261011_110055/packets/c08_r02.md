## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about a time he failed.

### A

🐾 Here's a notable instance.

While leading a global platform modernization for a **Fortune 500 financial services client**, Matt encountered a significant failure due to assumptions about the infrastructure. He assumed that IAM controls, environment boundaries, and data masking were correctly set up, based on inherited documentation from prior phases. This oversight led to an offshore tester accidentally triggering an email job in the UAT environment, which sent unmasked production data to thousands of real clients.

Matt took accountability for the incident and led the team through a root cause analysis and remediation process. This experience taught him that assumptions are risks in disguise. He learned the importance of validating IAM configurations, ensuring environment verification, and implementing PII scrubbing as day-one leadership responsibilities in his delivery approach. Despite the systemic nature of the failure, the client demanded the removal of the tester from the project, reinforcing Matt's belief that leaders own the environment their teams operate in.
