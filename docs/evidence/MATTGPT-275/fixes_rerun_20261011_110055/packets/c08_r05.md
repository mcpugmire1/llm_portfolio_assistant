## Turn 1. Visitor: Tell me about a time he failed.

### A

🐾 Here's a story about a challenging experience Matt faced.

During a global platform modernization delivery for a **Fortune 500 financial services client**, Matt encountered a significant failure. He was the tech lead responsible for ensuring a safe environment setup across multiple environments: dev, QA, UAT, and production. Under delivery pressure, he assumed that the IAM controls, environment segregation, and downstream access configurations were correct, relying on inherited documentation without verifying its accuracy.

This oversight led to an incident where an offshore tester accidentally triggered an email job in UAT, sending communications containing unmasked production data to thousands of real clients. The root cause was insufficient IAM checks, unclear environment boundaries, and unmasked production data in downstream environments—assumptions Matt had made without verification.

Matt took accountability for the incident and led the team through root cause analysis and remediation. He learned that assumptions are risks in disguise and that IAM validation, environment verification, PII scrubbing, and risk ownership are critical leadership responsibilities from day one. This experience reinforced his belief that leaders must own the environments their teams operate in, and he made these practices foundational in his delivery approach moving forward.
