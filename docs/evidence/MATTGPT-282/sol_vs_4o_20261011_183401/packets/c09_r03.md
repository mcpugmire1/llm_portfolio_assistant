Matt led payments data migration without disruption to live payment operations, preserving client configurations, entitlements, transaction histories, and compliance traceability. His user administration engine replaced individual permission changes with bulk grouping and role-based controls, reducing entitlement management from days to minutes. ACCESS launched in **April 2011**.

At **RBC**, from **June 2012 to June 2013**, the work focused on cross-border payment workflow design for **RBC** Express. Fragmented systems, manual processes, bulk-processing delays, and missing real-time FX tools were the starting point. Matt mapped initiation through settlement and reconciliation, partnered with the FX team, and designed real-time FX integration, automated alerts, recurring payments, Interac e-Transfer workflows, and batch processing.
