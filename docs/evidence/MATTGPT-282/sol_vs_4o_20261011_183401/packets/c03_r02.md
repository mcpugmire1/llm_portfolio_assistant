Matt made intent explicit, pushed decisions to the people closest to the work, and used direct feedback and open communication. He grounded engineering work in automation, continuous delivery, and quality-first practices. At **RBC**, he presented a data-migration strategy to senior leadership that the client adopted; at **Fiserv**, he brought an accessibility manifesto to IT leadership, the steering committee, and delivery teams, who adopted it as an ongoing development standard.
