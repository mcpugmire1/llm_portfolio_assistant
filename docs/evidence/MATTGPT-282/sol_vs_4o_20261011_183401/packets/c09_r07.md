He also delivered the centralized payments gateway for message transformation, protocol translation, and routing across **12 countries**, connecting ACCESS to clearing networks and internal compliance, settlement, and reporting systems. Payment-type-specific controls distinguished permissions such as wire approval from FX initiation. Bulk permission changes and a centralized administration dashboard reduced entitlement management from days to minutes.

Matt led the payments data migration, preserving client configurations, entitlements, transaction histories, and compliance traceability **without disruption to live payment operations**. ACCESS and its Integrated Global Payments Interface launched in **April 2011**. He also owned production support and critical-incident response.

At **RBC**, fragmented systems, manual processes, bulk-payment delays, and missing real-time FX tools affected **RBC** Express users. Matt designed workflows from initiation through settlement and reconciliation, partnering with the FX team on currency conversion and transaction visibility. His implementation blueprint covered real-time FX, automated alerts, recurring payments, Interac e-Transfer, and batch processing. Revised cutoff processes reduced wire-transfer delays; training materials supported business clients’ multi-currency account management.
