## Turn 1. Visitor: How did Matt calculate ROI?

### A

🐾 Here's how Matt assessed the investment and measured the return.

The stories describe cost-to-fix versus benefit comparisons, delivery measures, and financial tracking—not a single ROI formula or a reported ROI percentage. His work covered Execution & Delivery, Strategic & Advisory, and Org & Working-Model Transformation.

At **RBC**, leadership needed to decide whether Bottomline WebSeries could support its Day**2Day** Corporate Banking platform before committing further investment. Matt conducted code reviews, stakeholder interviews, and a structured architecture assessment. He worked with the enterprise architecture team on target-system models with a transparent cost-to-fix versus benefit comparison, then held workshops with leadership to connect business goals with the technical findings. The assessment found that WebSeries lacked the required modularity. **RBC** avoided that platform investment and redirected technical resources; the story does not assign a dollar value to the avoided spend.

At **Accenture’s** Cloud Innovation Centers, Matt addressed concerns about pair programming costs by presenting velocity gains and quality improvements that offset investment. He brought product, design, and engineering into balanced teams and reported **4X** faster velocity, **10-person** teams delivering equivalent output to traditional **20-person** teams, and zero-defect deployments. He also owned the centers’ budgets, costs, and P&L. Those are reported delivery and business measures, not a calculated ROI percentage.

For engagement economics, he tracked forecasts, actuals, variance, contingency, staffing costs, and scope changes. On the **Fiserv** recovery, an **\$8.5M** program finished **3%** under budget—**\$255K** in preserved margin—with **\$500K** in contractual penalties avoided and a **\$3M** extension. These were separate financial outcomes, not one combined ROI figure.

### B

These financial outcomes were directly tied to the disciplined management of engagement margin and resource optimization, demonstrating a clear ROI from the transformation efforts.

By focusing on delivery excellence and aligning solutions with client needs, Matt drove over **\$100M** in repeatable business through the CICs, further underscoring the ROI of his strategic initiatives.
