## Turn 1. Visitor: Compare his Fiserv and JP Morgan ACCESS work.

### A

🐾 Here's a great comparison of Matt's work with **Fiserv** and **JP Morgan**.

At **Fiserv**, Matt was instrumental in transforming the white-label card portal, which was a shared platform for major retailers like Home Depot and Citi. The portal faced significant challenges, including compliance with ADA and AODA standards, which required unique branding and compliance for each client. Matt took on the role of ADA Subject Matter Expert, leading UI refactoring and defining accessibility standards. This effort resulted in an ADA/AODA-compliant portal across all client brands, mitigating legal risks and allowing the platform to support multiple brands from a single codebase. Additionally, he modernized the DevOps processes, migrating the team from SVN to Git, implementing continuous integration with Hudson, and establishing quality gates with SonarQube. These changes improved the lead time from weeks to days and increased deployment frequency.

In contrast, at **JP Morgan**, Matt led the development of the ACCESS Next Generation platform, a flagship corporate digital banking portal for treasury services. This initiative required building bespoke payment modules and integrating them with JP Morgan's existing systems. Matt managed a blended team of over **40 members**, focusing on designing modules for ACH, wire, and FX payments, along with implementing a robust security layer and real-time global reporting. The ACCESS platform consolidated disparate regional systems into a unified global portal, serving over **135,000** clients across **180+ countries**. It became recognized as the #1 digital channel by Coalition Greenwich, highlighting its success in transforming JP Morgan's corporate banking experience.

He coordinated legacy-data verification with compliance teams, introduced compliance dashboards, and ran cross-team incident response and daily leadership calls during production issues.

**Fiserv** delivery stabilized within **3 months**; the program finished **3% under budget**, met **47 of 47** acceptance criteria with **zero critical defects** at launch, and avoided **\$500K** in penalties. Teams independently maintained accessibility compliance. ACCESS launched in **April 2011**, serving **135,000+** clients across **180+** countries; the gateway unified regional payment connections, and legacy migration completed without disruption to live payments.
