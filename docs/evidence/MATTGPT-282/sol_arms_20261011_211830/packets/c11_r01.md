🐾 Here’s how Matt scaled the Cloud Innovation Center.

Matt’s work combined Talent & Enablement with Org & Working-Model Transformation. At **Accenture**, he built the CIC to **150+ practitioners** across Atlanta and Tampa. The stories don’t confirm the starting count of **4**. Pair programming, TDD, continuous integration, and continuous delivery became standard practices. Retrospectives, psychological safety, and decisions pushed to the people closest to the work supported experimentation. Team scorecards tracked lead time, cycle time, throughput, team stability, and backlog health rather than velocity alone.

The CIC reached **150+ professionals**, with **near-zero attrition in the first 2 years** and multiple hires promoted. Teams consistently developed MVPs within weeks. Once teams embraced lean product and modern engineering practices, CIC delivery accelerated **4x**.
