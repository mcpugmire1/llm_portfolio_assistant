### A

🐾 Here’s how Matt compared investment with results.

Matt used cost-to-fix versus benefit comparisons, delivery benchmarks, and program financials. His stories do not specify a formal ROI formula, a payback period, or a calculated ROI percentage.

Those were separate financial outcomes—not a single ROI percentage, and not figures Matt combined into one return calculation.
