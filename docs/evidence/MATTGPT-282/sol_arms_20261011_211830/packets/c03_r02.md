On financial-services engagements, Matt integrated security requirements into the Definition of Done, assigned security-focused anchor engineers, and made risk tradeoffs visible to leadership. Those teams achieved **100% compliance** with PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOX. At the CIC, teams achieved **4x delivery acceleration** after embracing lean product and modern engineering practices.

### B

🐾 Yes—the initial approach at **Norfolk Southern** didn’t work as intended.

In early **2020**, Matt launched the Cloud Innovation Center’s first major engagement: modernizing **Norfolk Southern’s** legacy Revenue and Shipment Planning platform. He brought in a balanced team and emphasized test-driven development. A couple of months in, as the pandemic shifted work to remote, the client reported rising defects—even though the dashboards showed near-complete test coverage. The credibility hit landed in front of both the client and **Accenture** account leadership.

Matt found that an engineer was building from his own interpretation rather than the backlog story, then writing tests after the code. Coverage looked complete while defects went undetected. The backlog was also part of the problem: vague stories lacked defined scenarios and a definition of done. The team structure and stated engineering practices had not prevented those gaps.

He took ownership rather than defending the metrics. Matt ran a bottom-up audit of the codebase and test suite and showed the gaps openly. He rebuilt the backlog with SMART, testable stories and brought in frontend and backend TDD experts from Columbus to work alongside the team, redoing code and test cases.

He then added sustained, hands-on coaching: boot camps, coding katas, practice learning sessions, and pairing with subject-matter experts through an “I do, we do, you do” progression. He also restructured agile ceremonies and delivered product management and SMART story training.

Defect rates dropped, and the client stopped finding defects the dashboard could not see. Client confidence was restored through transparency and visible improvement. The story does not provide a numerical defect reduction.
