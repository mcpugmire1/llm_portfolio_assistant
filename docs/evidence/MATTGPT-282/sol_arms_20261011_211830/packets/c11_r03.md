## Turn 1. Visitor: How did Matt scale engineering teams from 4 to 150+ people?

### A

🐾 Here’s how Matt built the organization.

Matt’s scope grew from small delivery teams—including a **3–5-person** interface team at **AT&T**—to **150+ practitioners** at **Accenture**. The Cloud Innovation Centers themselves grew from a **10-person** pilot, rather than a **4-person** starting team. In Atlanta and Tampa, the challenge was recruiting people who often lacked hands-on cloud-native or Agile experience while building an organization without adding middle-management layers.

He kept the organization deliberately flat: **3** functional practice leads plus **8–10** client delivery leads reported directly to him. Engineers, product managers, and designers worked in balanced teams, with an experienced engineer serving as a team anchor. Monday allocation reviews brought Matt, practice leads, and client leads together to assign practitioners across engagements and internal training based on needs, skills, and location.

He built a hiring engine across **4** channels: internal bench search, campus recruiting, contract-to-hire, and job requisitions. Engineers completed hands-on TDD pairing auditions; product managers and designers completed live scoping exercises. Interviewers recorded independent votes, and Matt held the closing interview and made every final hiring decision. During scaling pushes, **15–20 auditions per week** produced **4–6 net-new practitioners per week**, with interview workloads capped to protect delivery.

New joiners received tailored enablement, then rotated through pairing with experienced practitioners. Training covered user-centered design, lean product development, pair programming, TDD, and CI/CD. Skills matrices and the “I do, we do, you do” learning model tracked progression toward independent delivery.

Because managers did not sit on daily teams, Matt ran monthly feedback capture with anchors and leads using evidence from recent pair partners. He also facilitated promotion calibration, allocated compensation, and owned remediation decisions.

Over about **4 years**, the centers reached **150+ practitioners**, with **near-zero attrition in the first 2 years** and multiple hires promoted. Their balanced teams delivered **4X faster velocity** and **zero-defect deployments**.
