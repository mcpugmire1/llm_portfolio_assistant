### B

🐾 Here’s an example from **Norfolk Southern**.

In early **2020**, Matt’s Cloud Innovation Center team was modernizing the legacy Revenue and Shipment Planning platform. Pairs rotated daily, newer engineers worked with seasoned practitioners, and test cases came before the solution—a step checked at standup. The client stopped finding defects the dashboard could not see. Defect rates dropped, client confidence returned, and **the failure mode did not recur**. Pairing and test-first remained the team’s default after the outside experts went home.
